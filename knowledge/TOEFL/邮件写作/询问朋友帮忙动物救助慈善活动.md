# 题目

#### **Situation**

You are organizing a charity event at your university to raise funds for a local animal shelter. You need volunteers to help with various tasks. You know that your friend, Emma, has experience in organizing events and would be a great help.

#### **Task**

Write an email to Emma. In your email, do the following:

· Describe the charity event and explain the type of support you need.

· Ask her if she would be willing to volunteer and specify the tasks she could assist with.

· Explain how her help would benefit human members of the community as well as animals.

**To:** Emma

**Subject:** Request for volunteering at charity event
Your Response:

# 我的回答

Dear Emma,  
I hope this email finds you well. I'm writing to tell you something about a charity event.  
I'm organizing a charity event at my unisversity to raise funds for a local animal shelter. Therefore, I need volunteers to help me with various tasks, which include helping to promote the activity and find more people to participate in it. Besides, I need assistant on sorting out the funds we raise and make a clear chart.  
For this reason, I'm writing to ask you if you can help me in the charity event, because you're an experience person in organizing events. I'm pretty sure you'll be a great help for me if you would like to promote this activity to more people. You can make speeches about animal protection or do something useful.  
I will be grateful if you make efforts in the event, which will ease the burden of our community members. In addition, our funds will be donated to a local animal shelter, which can help it to save homeless animals and take care for them. It's meaningful to do this kind of activity. I'm looking foward to your reply!  
Yours,  
Tom
