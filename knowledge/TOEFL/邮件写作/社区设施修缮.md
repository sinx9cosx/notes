# 题目

You are a resident in a local community and have noticed that some of the public facilities in your neighborhood are not well-maintained. For instance, the playground equipment is damaged, and the benches in the park are broken. These issues could pose safety risks for residents, especially children and elderly individuals. You decide to write an email to the community responsible person to address these concerns.  
  
Write an email to the community manager. In your email, do the following:  
- Point out the problem with the current state of the public facilities.  
- Describe the impact it might have on the community members.  
- Request that the management take necessary actions to repair and maintain these facilities.

# 我的回答

Dear Sir or Madam,
I hope this email finds you well. I'm Tom, a resident in this community. I'm writing to inform you a problem on public facilities in our neighborhood. I have noticed that the playground equipment is damaged, which causes inconvenience to exercisers. Yesterday when I came back and planned to use pull-up bar, I was disappointed to find it shaking. More severely, it can pose safety risks for residents, especially children and elderly individuals. Because they may move slowly when facing danger. 
Besides, the benches in the park are broken. It's dangerous to sit down directly without carefully check. People will get hurt by the broken benches and the rust can cause infection through the scar. 
Therefore, I sincerely require you to take nacessary actions to repair and maintain these facilities. Except for this, it may help to check these facilities once a month. In this way, you will find problems on time and make community better. 
Best regards, 
Tom

# 改正版（2026-10-12，AI参考估分 4/6）

Dear Sir or Madam,

I hope this email finds you well. I'm Tom, a resident in this community. I'm writing to inform you **of** a problem **with** public facilities in our neighborhood. I have noticed that the playground equipment is damaged, which causes inconvenience to exercisers. Yesterday when I came back and planned to use **the** pull-up bar, I was disappointed to find it shaking. **More seriously**, it can pose safety risks for residents, especially children and elderly individuals **, because** they may move slowly when facing danger.
Besides, the benches in the park are broken. It's dangerous to sit down directly without carefully **checking**. People will get hurt by the broken benches and the rust can cause infection through the **wound**.
Therefore, I sincerely **request** you to take **necessary** actions to repair and maintain these facilities. **In addition**, it may help to check these facilities once a month. In this way, you will find problems **in time** and make **the** community better.
Best regards,
Tom

估分说明：三项任务（指出问题、描述影响、请求修缮）全部完成且有个人经历支撑；介词、冠词、拼写、语域等错误约12处，意思清楚，定档4/6。

纠错要点：
- 拼写：nacessary → necessary（拼写错误）
- 语法冠词：inform you a problem → inform you of a problem（inform sb of sth 固定搭配）
- 语法冠词：a problem on public facilities → a problem with public facilities（problem 与 with 搭配）
- 语法冠词：use pull-up bar → use the pull-up bar（缺冠词）
- 语法冠词：individuals. Because they may move → individuals, because they may move（Because 从句不能独立成句，与前句合并）
- 语法冠词：without carefully check → without carefully checking（without 后接动名词）
- 语法冠词：make community better → make the community better（缺冠词）
- 搭配用词：More severely → More seriously（修饰"问题更严重"应用 seriously）
- 搭配用词：Except for this → In addition（except for 意为"除……之外"，此处想说"此外还有"）
- 搭配用词：find problems on time → find problems in time（on time 意为"准时"，in time 意为"及时"）
- 逻辑一致性：infection through the scar → infection through the wound（scar 是愈合后的疤痕，伤口应说 wound）
- 语域与格式：I sincerely require you → I sincerely request you（require 带命令语气，向管理者提请求应用 request）
