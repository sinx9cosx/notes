# 题目

You are a resident in a local community and have noticed that some of the public facilities in your neighborhood are not well-maintained. For instance, the playground equipment is damaged, and the benches in the park are broken. These issues could pose safety risks for residents, especially children and elderly individuals. You decide to write an email to the community responsible person to address these concerns.  
  
Write an email to the community manager. In your email, do the following:  
- Point out the problem with the current state of the public facilities.  
- Describe the impact it might have on the community members.  
- Request that the management take necessary actions to repair and maintain these facilities.

# 我的作文

Dear Sir or Madam,
I hope this email finds you well. I'm Tom, a resident in this community. I'm writing to inform you a problem on public facilities in our neighborhood. I have noticed that the playground equipment is damaged, which causes inconvenience to exercisers. Yesterday when I came back and planned to use <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">pull-up bar</mark>, I was disappointed to find it shaking. More severely, it can pose safety risks for residents, especially children and elderly individuals. Because they may move slowly when facing danger. 
Besides, the benches in the park are broken. It's dangerous to sit down directly without carefully check. People will get hurt by the broken benches and the rust can cause infection through the scar. 
Therefore, I sincerely require you to take nacessary actions to repair and maintain these facilities. Except for this, it may help to check these facilities once a month. In this way, you will find problems on time and make community better. 
Best regards, 
Tom

# 修改

Dear Sir or Madam,

I hope this email finds you well. I'm Tom, a resident in this community. I'm writing to report a problem with the public facilities in our neighborhood. I have noticed that some of the playground equipment is damaged, which makes it difficult for residents to exercise. Yesterday, when I came back and planned to use the pull-up bar, I was disappointed to find it shaking. More importantly, this could pose a safety risk for residents, especially children and elderly individuals, because they may not be able to react quickly in an emergency.
Besides, the benches in the park are broken. Sitting on these broken benches could cause injuries, and the exposed metal may create additional safety concerns for residents.
Therefore, I sincerely request that you take the necessary actions to repair and maintain these facilities. In addition, it would be helpful to check these facilities regularly, such as once a month. In this way, problems can be found and addressed in time, helping to keep our community safe and well-maintained.
Best regards,  
Tom

# 范文

Dear Community Manager,
I am writing to bring your attention to several problems with the public facilities in our neighborhood. Recently, I noticed that some of the playground equipment, including the pull-up bar, has been damaged. In addition, several benches in the park are broken and have exposed metal parts.
These problems may create safety risks for community members. For example, damaged playground equipment could cause injuries to children and other residents who use it for exercise. The broken benches may also make it difficult for elderly residents to sit and rest safely.
I would therefore appreciate it if the management could arrange for the damaged facilities to be repaired or replaced as soon as possible. It may also be helpful to inspect the facilities regularly so that similar problems can be identified and addressed before they become serious.
Thank you for your attention to this matter. I look forward to seeing these improvements in our community.
Best regards,  
Tom
