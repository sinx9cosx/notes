# 题目

You are interested in a summer internship program at a company.  
  
**Write an email to HR Manager. In your email, do the following:**  
  
-Express appreciation for being considered.  
-Describe the part of the program that interests you most.  
-Ask what documents you need to submit.  
  
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

# 我的答案

Dear Sir or Madam, 
I hope this email finds you well. I am interested in the summer intership program at the company, and I'm writing to ask something about it.
First of all, I really appreciate it for being considered to the program. Thanks for giving me a chance to improve myself. 
The most interesting part of the program for me is product reseach and develpment. It's exciting for me to develop new type of products with my professional knowledge, and I will be proud if my products are used by many people. Besides, I can communicate with collegues to have good inspiration, which is both beneficial for myself and for the company.
Last but not least, could you please tell me what documents do I need to submit? I will get ready with them and prepare for the coming work. Thanks for your time, and I look forward to your reply.
Sincerely,
Tom

## 错误对照表

| 原文表达 | 问题类型 | 改正/改进 |
|---|---|---|
| Dear Sir or Madam | 称呼偏过时、泛化（收件人为 HR Manager，用 Hiring Manager 更得体） | Dear Hiring Manager |
| intership | 拼写错误 | internship |
| at the company | 表达视角不当（收件人视角应称 your company） | at your company |
| I'm writing to ask something about it | 动词搭配不当（ask 后不宜直接接 something，且未说明要问什么） | I'm writing to ask a few questions about it |
| appreciate it for being considered to the program | 代词赘余（appreciate 直接接动名词）+ 介词错误（considered to→for） | appreciate being considered for the program |
| Thanks for giving me a chance to improve myself | 与上一句 appreciate 连续致谢、语义重复 | 与上一句合并为 "appreciate being considered for the program and the chance to improve myself" |
| reseach | 拼写错误 | research |
| develpment | 拼写错误 | development |
| new type of products | 单复数不一致（type 与 products 数不一致） | new types of products |
| I will be proud if | 条件句语气不当（未定情形用 will 过于笃定，建议 would） | I would be proud if |
| collegues | 拼写错误 | colleagues |
| to have good inspiration | 搭配不自然（have good inspiration 非地道搭配） | to gain fresh inspiration |
| which is both beneficial for myself and for the company | 平行结构 + 代词错误（both...for myself...and for...不平；myself→me） | which is beneficial for both me and the company |
| what documents do I need to submit | 间接疑问句语序错误（tell me 后应用陈述语序） | what documents I need to submit |
| get ready with them | 表达不自然（get ready with them 生硬、有歧义） | get them ready |
| the coming work | 语义偏差（实习尚未确定，coming work 暗示已入职） | the upcoming internship |

## 改正后的作文

Dear Hiring Manager,

I hope this email finds you well. I am interested in the summer internship program at your company, and I'm writing to ask a few questions about it.

First of all, I really appreciate being considered for the program and the chance to improve myself.

The most interesting part of the program for me is product research and development. It's exciting for me to develop new types of products with my professional knowledge, and I would be proud if my products are used by many people. Besides, I can communicate with colleagues to gain fresh inspiration, which is beneficial for both me and the company.

Last but not least, could you please tell me what documents I need to submit? I will get them ready and prepare for the upcoming internship. Thanks for your time, and I look forward to your reply.

Sincerely,
Tom
