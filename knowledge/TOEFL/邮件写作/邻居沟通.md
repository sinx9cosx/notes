# 题目

You recently moved into a new apartment, but a problem has arisen. Your next-door neighbor often plays loud music late at night, which disrupts your sleep and affects your ability to focus during the day. You need to address this issue to find a resolution.  
  
Write an email to your neighbor. In your email, do the following:  
- Explain who you are and why you're writing  
- Describe the issue of loud music and how it is affecting you  
- Request a possible solution to ensure quiet during late hours

# 我的回答

Dear neighbor,
I moved to this apartment recently and became your new neighbor. I'm writing to discuss a problem about your music and hope we can reach a consensus. 
It seems that you often play music at night, which disrupts my sleep and affects my ability to focus during the day. When I'm about to sleep, the loud music often keeps me awake. Even if I fall asleep reluctantly, I would be woken up by noise. In consequence, I couldn't pay attention to classes in school, which makes it difficult for me to catch up with my teachers. 
Thus, I would greatly appreciate it if you could keep quiet after 10 p.m. Hope you can consider this request and find a possible solution. Thanks for your time! 
Sincerely, 
Tom

# 改正版（2026-10-12，AI参考估分 4/6）

Dear **Neighbor**,

I moved to this apartment recently and became your new neighbor. I'm writing to discuss a problem **with** your music and hope we can reach **an agreement**.
It seems that you often play music at night, which disrupts my sleep and affects my ability to focus during the day. When I'm about to sleep, the loud music often keeps me awake. **Even when** I **finally** fall asleep, I **am** woken up by **the** noise. In consequence, I **can't** pay attention to classes in school, which makes it difficult for me to **keep up with** my teachers.
Thus, I would greatly appreciate it if you could keep quiet after 10 p.m. Hope you can consider this request and find a possible solution. Thanks for your time!
Sincerely,
Tom

估分说明：三项任务（自我介绍与来意、描述问题及影响、请求解决方案）全部完成且影响描述具体；时态混乱、搭配等语言错误约9处，定档4/6。

纠错要点：
- 语法冠词：Even if I fall asleep → Even when I fall asleep（反复发生的事实应用 when，if 表假设）
- 语法冠词：I would be woken up → I am woken up（时态与全文现在时不一致）
- 语法冠词：by noise → by the noise（特指深夜音乐声，加冠词）
- 语法冠词：I couldn't pay attention → I can't pay attention（描述当前持续影响，用现在时）
- 搭配用词：a problem about your music → a problem with your music（problem 与 with 搭配）
- 搭配用词：fall asleep reluctantly → finally fall asleep（reluctantly 意为"不情愿地"，作者本意是"好不容易入睡"）
- 搭配用词：catch up with my teachers → keep up with my teachers（catch up with 意为"追上/叙旧"，此处应为"跟上"）
- 语域与格式：Dear neighbor → Dear Neighbor（称呼首字母大写）
- 语域与格式：reach a consensus → reach an agreement（consensus 对日常邻里沟通而言过正式）
