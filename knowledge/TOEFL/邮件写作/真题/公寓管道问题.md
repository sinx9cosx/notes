# 题目

Situation  
You recently moved into a new apartment off campus. Since moving in, you have experienced several plumbing issues. You need to address this issue with the building manager, Mr. Thompson, and request a resolution.  
Task Mr. Thompson, and request a resolution.  
Task  
Write an email to Mr. Thompson. In your email, do the following:  
· Describe the plumbing issues you are experiencing.  
· Explain how the issues are affecting you.  
· Request a resolution to the problem.  
To: Mr. Thompson  
Subject: Plumbing Issues in Apartment  
Your Response:

# 回答

Dear Mr. Thompson,  
I am the resident who recently moved into the apartment. I'm writing to express my concern about several issues.  
Every night during my bedtime, I will hear a noise from my next door, which often erupts my sleep. As a result, I coudn't focus on my daily work.  
Besides, water leaks through my roof everyday, making my floor wet and slippery. It's dangerous for me to walk through there.  
I hope you can solute these problems. I appreciate it. Thank you!  
Sincerely,  
Tom

# 审题

plumbing issues＝房屋给排水、管道及相关设施的问题。

- **a leaking pipe**：水管漏水
    
- **a dripping faucet**：水龙头滴水
    
- **a clogged drain**：下水道堵塞
    
- **a clogged toilet**：马桶堵塞
    
- **low water pressure**：水压太低
    
- **no hot water**：没有热水
    
- **The toilet keeps running.**：马桶一直流水

# 改正

Dear Mr. Thompson,
I recently moved into Apartment 304, and I am writing to express my concern about several plumbing issues in my unit.
First, water is leaking from a pipe under the kitchen sink. In addition, the bathroom drain is clogged, so the water drains very slowly. As a result, it is difficult for me to use my kitchen and bathroom normally. The leaking water has also made the floor wet and slippery, which is dangerous.
Could you please arrange for a plumber to inspect and repair these problems as soon as possible? I am available after 3 p.m. on weekdays. Please let me know when the plumber can come.
Thank you for your help.  
Sincerely,
Tom

# 范文

Dear Mr. Thompson,  
I hope you are doing well. I recently moved into the apartment and have noticed several plumbing problems. The kitchen sink has been leaking continuously, and the water pressure in the bathroom shower is very low. In addition, the toilet occasionally takes a long time to drain.  
These issues have been quite inconvenient. The leaking sink has left water around the kitchen counter, and I am concerned that it could eventually cause damage to the cabinets. The low water pressure also makes it difficult to use the shower normally. Since I have just moved in, I would really appreciate having these problems addressed before they become more serious.  
Could you please arrange for a maintenance worker to inspect the plumbing and repair the issues as soon as possible? If someone needs to enter the apartment, please let me know in advance so that I can make sure I am available.  
Thank you for your attention to this matter. I look forward to hearing from you soon.  
Sincerely,  
[Your Name]
