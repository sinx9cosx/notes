# 题目

You recently dined at a local restaurant near campus and were impressed overall. However, you experienced some issues with the dining experience. You want to contact the restaurant manager, Ms. Miller, to address these issues.  
  
**Write an email to Ms. Miller. In your email, do the following:**  
  
-Describe what you enjoyed about your visit.  
-Explain the issues with the dining experience.  
-Suggest a solution for the issues you faced.  
  
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

# 回答

Dear Ms. Miller, 
I visited your restaurant recently. It was an enjoyable experience. I loved the quiet atmosphere, and the food <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">tastes</mark> good. 
However, I didn't bring enough money, so I didn't pay for my meal. Thanks for your understanding again. 
Could you please send my bill to me? Then I will pay my bill by card. Please let me know if you need further information from me. 
Best regards, 
Tom

# 范文

Dear Ms. Miller,
I am writing to share feedback on my visit to your restaurant last Friday evening. I was genuinely impressed by the warm atmosphere and the attentive service from our waiter, who **checked on us several times**. The **grilled salmon** I ordered was cooked perfectly, and the **lemon sauce** was delicious.
However, I did experience two issues. First, our table was near the kitchen door, and the constant traffic made it quite noisy. Second, my friend's pasta arrived almost **fifteen minutes after** the rest of our meals, so we could not eat together.
I would suggest adding a <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">partition</mark> near the kitchen entrance to reduce the noise, and having the kitchen coordinate orders for the same table so all dishes are served at once. I hope this feedback is helpful, and I look forward to dining with you again.
Sincerely,
A satisfied customer

# Issue 补充

> [!note] 用途
> 备考写作短语库：Issue → 解决办法

## 食物类
- 菜品与菜单不符 → 更新菜单照片与描述
- 太咸 / 太淡 / 没熟 / 烧焦 → 重做或更换菜品
- 食物中有异物 → 重做并免单
- 上桌时已凉 → 加快传菜速度
- 分量偏少 → 统一出餐标准
- 忌口 / 过敏需求未满足 → 下单时标注并确认

## 服务类
- 上菜太慢 → 高峰期加派人手
- 服务员态度差 → 加强员工培训
- 上错菜 / 漏单 → 下单后复述订单确认
- 难叫到服务员 → 高峰期增派人手
- 服务员不熟悉菜品 → 定期菜品培训

## 环境类
- 桌面脏 → 餐前加强清洁检查
- 餐具不干净 / 缺失 → 餐前检查并补齐
- 空调过冷 / 过热 → 调节室温
- 灯光太暗 → 调亮灯光
- 油烟味重 → 改善通风系统
- 预订后无座位 / 等位久 → 完善预订系统

## 账单类
- 账单多算钱 → 核对账单后退差价
- 刷卡机故障 → 备用 POS 机 / 提前告知顾客
- 菜价与标价不符 → 更新菜单价格
