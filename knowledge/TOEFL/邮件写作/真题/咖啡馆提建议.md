# 题目

You recently visited a new café that opened near your campus called Sunny Cafe. Although you generally enjoyed the cafe, you were disappointed with some issues.  
Task  
Write an email to Ms. Garcia. In your email, do the following:  
· Describe what you liked about your visit to the cafe.  
· Explain why you were disappointed with the limited selection of pastries.  
· Suggest a few types of pastries that could be added to the menu to improve the selection.  
To: Ms. Garcia  
Subject: Suggestions for expanding pastry selection  
Your Response:

# 回答

Dear Ms. Garcia,  
I really enjoy the cafe you opened. I love the quiet atmosphere in it, because I can do my homework efficiently. Also, the coffee tastes good, which quickly becomes one of my favorite drinks in school.  
However, I'm a little disappointed with the limited selection of pastries. I don't like fruit flavors because it's too sweet. But there's no other choices. That really bothers me.  
Could you please make some new types of pastries? For example, I believe pastries with chocolate and creams will be popular.  
Thanks for your time! I would appreciate it if you consider my advice.  
Sincerely,  
Tom

# 范文

Dear Ms. Garcia,
I hope you are doing well. I recently visited Sunny Cafe and really enjoyed my experience there. The atmosphere was warm and relaxing, and I especially liked the comfortable seating and friendly service. The coffee was also excellent, so I can definitely see myself coming back to the cafe.
One thing that was a little disappointing, however, was the limited selection of pastries. I visited in the afternoon, but there were only a couple of options available. Since many students like to have a snack with their coffee, I think having more choices would make the cafe even more appealing.
Perhaps you could consider adding some popular options such as croissants, blueberry muffins, and chocolate cake. It might also be nice to offer a few healthier choices, such as fruit scones or oatmeal cookies. A wider selection could attract more students and give regular customers more reasons to return.
Thank you for taking the time to consider my suggestions. I hope they are helpful, and I look forward to visiting Sunny Cafe again.
Best regards,  
[Your Name]
