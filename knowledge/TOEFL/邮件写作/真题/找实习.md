# 题目

You are a student who recently applied for a summer internship at a company. You have received an email from the personnel manager, Mr. Davis, asking you to provide additional documents and information to complete your application.

Task

Write an email to Mr. Davis. In your email, do the following:

·Thank him for considering your application.

·Explain what interests you most about the job for which you are applying.

·List the additional documents and information you are sending.

To:Mr. Davis

Subject:Summer internship application - additional documentsYour Response:

# 回答

Dear Mr. Davis,  
I hope this email finds you well. Thank you for considering my application, I'm glad to have a chance to work in your company.  
As for the interesting part of the job for me, I'd like to parcipate in the products research, which is match for my major. Besides, it's exciting for me to see that my hard work turns into market products and makes people's life more convenient. Therefore, I would be appreciate it if you give me a chance to do researches on improving the technology of products.  
Here are my additional documents and information sent in the additional files, including my certificates and research experiences before. Thanks for your time! I'm looking forward to your reply!  
Sincerely,  
Tom

# 范文

Dear Mr. Davis,  
 Thank you for considering my application for the summer internship. I am particularly interested in this position because it would allow me to gain practical experience in marketing and learn how professionals develop strategies for attracting customers. I also believe that the internship would help me apply what I have learned in my university courses to real-world situations.  
As requested, I am sending my updated resume, academic transcript, and cover letter. I have also included my current phone number and email address in case you need any additional information from me.  
Please let me know if there are any other documents or details that I should provide. Thank you again for your time and consideration. I look forward to hearing from you.  
Sincerely,  
Your Name
