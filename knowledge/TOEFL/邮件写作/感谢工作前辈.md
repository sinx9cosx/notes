# 题目

You recently started a new job on campus and have been assigned a mentor, Ms. Johnson, who has given you valuable guidance and advice. You want to express your appreciation.  
  
Write an email to Ms. Johnson. In your email, do the following:
  
-Express your appreciation for her guidance and advice.  
-Describe how her guidance has helped you in your new job.  
-Mention what you look forward to learning from her.  
  
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

# 我的回答

Dear Ms. Johnson,
I hope this email finds you well. I'm writing to express my appreciation for valuable guidance and advice that you have given to me. I have learned a lot from your structured teaching. 
When I came to this job at first, I was totally confused about what I was supposed to do. I remember that you teached me step by step, and patiently answered my questions no matter how easy they were. Even if I didn't know how to use a printer, you helped me to put papers in and explained the procedure. When I made mistakes, you always encouraged me to try again without blame. Besides, you have provided me your own experience on facing the same problem, which helped me to deal it more smoothly. 
I'm really grateful for your teaching, and I'm looking forward to your further guidance about how to balance work and life. I hope that I can become a good person like you. 
Best regards,
Tom

# 改正版（2026-10-12，AI参考估分 4/6）

Dear Ms. Johnson,

I hope this email finds you well. I'm writing to express my appreciation for **the** valuable guidance and advice that you have given to me. I have learned a lot from your structured teaching.
**When I first came to this job**, I was totally confused about what I was supposed to do. I remember that you **taught** me step by step, and patiently answered my questions no matter how easy they were. **Even when** I didn't know how to use a printer, you helped me to put **paper** in and explained the procedure. When I made mistakes, you always encouraged me to try again without blame. Besides, you have provided me **with** your own experience **in** facing **similar** problems, which helped me to deal **with** it more smoothly.
I'm really grateful for your **help**, and I'm looking forward to your further guidance about how to balance work and life. I hope that I can become **as professional and capable as you**.
Best regards,
Tom

纠错要点：
- 词形词性：teached → taught（teach 过去式为不规则变化 taught）
- 词形词性：put papers in → put paper in（paper 作"纸张"讲不可数）
- 语法冠词：for valuable guidance → for the valuable guidance（有定语从句限定，加 the）
- 语法冠词：Even if I didn't know → Even when I didn't know（反复发生的事实应用 when）
- 语法冠词：provided me your own experience → provided me with your own experience（provide sb with sth 缺 with）
- 语法冠词：deal it more smoothly → deal with it more smoothly（deal 不及物，须加 with）
- 搭配用词：experience on facing the same problem → experience in facing similar problems（experience 后接 in doing；"the same problem" 前文无对应，改 similar）
- 搭配用词：When I came to this job at first → When I first came to this job（at first 位置不当）
- 搭配用词：grateful for your teaching → grateful for your help（与前文 teaching 重复）
- 逻辑一致性：become a good person like you → become as professional and capable as you（good person 偏道德评价，作者本意是"像您一样专业能干"；内容级修正）
