# 题目

You recently started a new job on campus and have been assigned a mentor, Ms. Johnson, who has given you valuable guidance and advice. You want to express your appreciation.  
  
Write an email to Ms. Johnson. In your email, do the following:
  
-Express your appreciation for her guidance and advice.  
-Describe how her guidance has helped you in your new job.  
-Mention what you look forward to learning from her.  
  
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

# 我的回答

Dear Ms. Johnson,
I hope this email finds you well. I'm writing to express my appreciation for valuable guidance and advice that you have given to me. I have learned a lot from your structured teaching. 
When I came to this job at first, I was totally confused about what I was supposed to do. I remember that you teached me step by step, and patiently answered my questions no matter how easy they were. Even if I didn't know how to use a printer, you helped me to put papers in and explained the procedure. When I made mistakes, you always encouraged me to try again without blame. Besides, you have provided me your own experience on facing the same problem, which helped me to deal it more smoothly. 
I'm really grateful for your teaching, and I'm looking forward to your further guidance about how to balance work and life. I hope that I can become a good person like you. Best regards，
Tom

# 评分与改正

## 评分结论

- 内容相关性：4/5。三个任务点（表达感谢、说明指导如何帮助自己、表达期待学习的内容）全部覆盖，且有打印机、犯错被鼓励、工作生活平衡等具体事例支撑；但事例细节展开有限，故未给满分。
- 结构组织：4/5。称呼、问候、开头致谢、正文举例、结尾敬语与落款齐全，段落分工清晰；扣分点为结尾敬语格式错误（"Best regards，"与正文同段且用全角逗号），且末段信息略杂。
- 语言运用：3/5。存在不规则动词（teached）、介词缺失（deal it）、可数性（papers）、冠词、连词（Even if）、动词搭配（provided...experience on）等多类错误，约 160 词篇幅内错误密度偏高；但意思传达基本不受影响，句式有一定变化。
- 综合原始分：(4+4+3)/3 ≈ 3.7，按半分制四舍五入记为 3.5/5。
- 换算总分：按新托福写作 0–5 原始分至 0–30 分的换算关系，3.5 约对应 21–22，取 22/30。
- 折算说明：新托福改革（2023 年 7 月起）后写作部分为「综合写作 + 学术讨论」两题，已无邮件题型；本评分按新托福写作评分维度（内容相关性、结构组织、语言运用）折算，仅作练习参考。

## 错误对照表

| 原文表达 | 问题类型 | 改正/改进 |
|---|---|---|
| teached | 不规则动词过去式错误 | taught |
| helped me to deal it | 缺介词（deal 不及物，须接 with） | helped me deal with it（范文中随前文复数改为 helped me handle them） |
| provided me your own experience on facing the same problem | 动词搭配 + 介词错误（experience 后接 in doing）；"the same problem" 无先行词 | shared your own experience in dealing with similar problems |
| Even if | 连词误用（if 表假设；此处是反复发生的事实，应表"每当"） | Even when |
| for valuable guidance | 缺冠词（后有定语从句限定，须用 the） | for the valuable guidance |
| helped me to put papers in | paper 不可数误加复数 + 表达生硬 | showed me how to load the paper |
| no matter how easy they were | 搭配润色（easy 可用，simple 与 questions 搭配更自然） | no matter how simple they were |
| become a good person like you | 表达笼统、语义偏差（good person 偏道德评价） | become as professional and capable as you |
| Best regards， | 全角逗号；敬语未单独成行（Tom 已换行，问题在敬语本身） | Best regards, 与 Tom 各占一行 |
| teaching 出现两次（structured teaching、your teaching）；"I have learned a lot" 与 "I'm really grateful for your teaching" 重复 | 用词重复 | 换用 guidance/help 等；两处感谢重复表达分别改写去重 |
| When I came to this job at first | 表达不自然（润色） | When I first started this job |

## 改正后的作文

Dear Ms. Johnson,

I hope this email finds you well. I'm writing to express my appreciation for the valuable guidance and advice that you have given me. I have learned a great deal from your structured guidance.

When I first started this job, I was totally confused about what I was supposed to do. I remember that you taught me step by step and patiently answered my questions, no matter how simple they were. Even when I didn't know how to use a printer, you showed me how to load the paper and explained the procedure. When I made mistakes, you always encouraged me to try again without blame. Besides, you shared your own experience in dealing with similar problems, which helped me handle them more smoothly.

I'm truly grateful for all your help, and I look forward to learning more from you about how to balance work and life. I hope that one day I can become as professional and capable as you.

Best regards,
Tom
