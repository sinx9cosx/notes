# 题目

You are a student representative on your university's committee for improving campus facilities. The university is planning to open a new cafe on campus, and the committee has asked for student suggestions regarding the menu and services that the cafe should provide to best meet the needs of students. Based on your discussions with other students, you have collected some ideas and need to communicate these to the committee. Write an email to the committee chair. In your email, do the following: - Summarize the primary feedback or preferences you gathered from the student. - Describe the menu and service features that you would like to recommend, and explain their benefits. - Request that your recommendations be considered for further discussion in the committee.

# 改正版（2026-10-12，AI参考估分 4/6）

Dear **Committee Chair**,

Hello! I'm writing to talk about **the** school's new cafe plan and I have collected some ideas as a student **representative**.
In summary, most students are in favor of opening a new cafe, because it's convenient to get drinks when **they feel** thirsty in school. Also, it offers a space for rest and communication.
**Based on the** suggestions from other students, as for the menu, I'd like to recommend traditional drinks like coffee, milk and juice. **They're** popular and healthy, **so** most students will love **them**. Additionally, I suggest that the cafe **sell** different food according to seasons. For example, selling ice cream in summer while selling hot chocolate in winter **would be** a success since students can cool down or warm up by eating these **foods**. When it comes to the service, I think the cafe can spare some space for group work discussion or **self-study**. Good environment is the key to **attracting** students.
All in all, **I hope** my recommendations **will** be considered for further discussion to make the cafe better. **Thank you for reading**!
Sincerely,
Tom

纠错要点：
- 拼写：representive → representative（拼写错误）
- 语域与格式：Dear committee → Dear Committee Chair（题目要求写给委员会主席，称呼应具体）；Thanks for your reading → Thank you for reading（your reading 搭配不自然）
- 语法冠词：school's new cafe plan → the school's new cafe plan（缺冠词）
- 语法冠词：when feeling thirsty → when they feel thirsty（悬垂结构，补出逻辑主语）
- 语法冠词：It's popular and healthy → They're popular and healthy（指代 drinks，应复数）；so that most students will love it → so most students will love them（so that 表目的，此处表结果）
- 语法冠词：the cafe can sell → the cafe sell（suggest 后宾语从句用虚拟语气，不加 can）
- 语法冠词：the key to attract students → the key to attracting students（the key to 后接动名词）
- 搭配用词：In consideration of → Based on the（consideration of 语义为"考虑到"，此处应为"根据"）
- 搭配用词：must be a success → would be a success（must 语气过绝对，与建议语境不符）
- 搭配用词：by eating these → by eating these foods（these 指代不明，补出名词）
- 词形词性：self study → self-study（连字符复合词）
