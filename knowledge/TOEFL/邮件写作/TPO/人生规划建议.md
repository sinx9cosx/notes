# 题目

You recently had a conversation with your friend Sarah, who is considering a new job opportunity in another city. Sarah is unsure about making the decision and asked for advice. In the past, you have discussed her career goals and personal priorities together, and now you want to provide thoughtful advice based on her current situation.  
  
Write an email to Sarah. In your email, do the following:  
- Explain your perspective on her situation.  
- Offer specific advice to help her make a decision.  
- Encourage her to weigh her options.

# 改正版（2026-10-12，AI参考估分 3.5/6）

Dear Sarah,

I hope you're doing well. Hearing that you're **considering** a new job opportunity, I'm happy to tell you my advice.
I remember that you always hold a positive attitude toward **your career, and** this new job offers **the right road** to achieve your goal. For your social life, **there's no need** to worry because you can communicate with friends online **conveniently**. Also we can go to see you in **our** free time.
Therefore, I **advise** you to take this chance. Although it takes time to adapt to **a** new environment, you can also enhance your professional skills rapidly under pressure.
Besides, exploring **a** new city and making friends can bring you fun! Lastly, I hope you can consider it carefully as it is a big deal for you. No matter what your **decision** is, I'll always support you. So just follow your heart and be brave. Hope my advice can help you.
Best regards,
Tina
