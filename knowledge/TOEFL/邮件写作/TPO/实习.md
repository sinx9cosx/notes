# 题目

You are interested in a summer internship program at a company.  
  
**Write an email to HR Manager. In your email, do the following:**  
  
-Express appreciation for being considered.  
-Describe the part of the program that interests you most.  
-Ask what documents you need to submit.  
  
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

# 我的回答

Dear Sir or Madam, 
I hope this email finds you well. I am interested in the summer intership program at the company, and I'm writing to ask something about it.
First of all, I really appreciate it for being considered to the program. Thanks for giving me a chance to improve myself. 
The most interesting part of the program for me is product reseach and develpment. It's exciting for me to develop new type of products with my professional knowledge, and I will be proud if my products are used by many people. Besides, I can communicate with collegues to have good inspiration, which is both beneficial for myself and for the company.
Last but not least, could you please tell me what documents do I need to submit? I will get ready with them and prepare for the coming work. Thanks for your time, and I look forward to your reply.
Sincerely,
Tom

First of all, I really appreciate **being considered for** the program **and the chance to improve myself**.
