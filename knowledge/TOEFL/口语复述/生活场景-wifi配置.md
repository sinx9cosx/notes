# 复述内容

Begin by plugging in the power cord.
Attach the internet cable as shown.
Adjust the antenna on the device for a stronger signal.
Check the indicator lights to ensure the router is working.
Log in to the system using your computer's keyboard.
You can then create a new safe and secure Wi-Fi password for your network.
If you experience issues with the connection, try restarting your computer.
