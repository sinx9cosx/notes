# 复述内容

Lightly wet your brush before starting to paint.
Add a small bit of color to your brush.
mix two or more paints to create additional colors.
Apply thin layers first to keep the paper from tearing.
Use a smaller brush to add fine details to the picture.
If a section looks too wet, blot gently to pick up any excess paint.
When the paiting is finished, blow warm air on it before moving or storing it.
