# 复述内容

Measue carefully before starting to mix.
Add yeast tea, small amount of warm.
Stir the wet and dry ingredients into a soft.
Press and fold repeatedly until the texture feels a allowed.
Let the dough rest in a warm place until it doubles inside.
After the loaf rises, bake until the top is brown and the center is.
When the bread is done, it on a raised stand so airflow beneath can cool it down.
