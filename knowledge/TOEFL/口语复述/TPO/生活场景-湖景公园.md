## 复述内容

Welcome to Lakeview Park.
The main lake is in the center.
Picnic areas stretch along the eastern shore.
Walking trails wind through dense forest nearby.
Rent a canoe at the west dock for water activities.
If you want to see wildlife, visit the observation deck near the forest edge.
Please ensure you follow park rules, including keeping the area litter free and respecting wildlife habitats.
