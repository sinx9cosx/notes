## 复述内容

Please ask staff if you need help finding items.
Don't forget to take your receipt before leaving checkout counters.
Because bakery products sell fast, you might want to get there early.
If you buy frozen goods, remember to use insulated bags for your trip home.
Weekly discounts apply only when you show membership cards at payment.
The section that displays imported snacks requires extra care when handling products.
Before we approve refund requests, staff must check original purchase receipts carefully.
