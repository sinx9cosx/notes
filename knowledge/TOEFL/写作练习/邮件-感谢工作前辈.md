# 题目

You recently started a new job on campus and have been assigned a mentor, Ms.Johnson, who has given you valuable guidance and advice. You want to express yourappreciation.
Write an email to Ms. Johnson. In your email, do the following:
- Describe specific ways her mentorship has helped you.
-Tell her which piece of advice was the most helpful to you and explain why.
- Ask for suggestions on how to improve your performance.
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

# 原文改写

Dear Ms. Johnson,

Thank you for your guidance in my job. I was really confused at first when I did my work, but you always taught me patiently. When I felt discouraged, you told me about your own experience to build up my confidence again.

The most helpful advice you gave me was the way to deal with mistakes. I learned how to face them positively and deal with them using various methods to reduce their influence. That helps me not only in this job, but also in my schoolwork.

In addition, I would appreciate it if you could give me some suggestions on how to improve my working efficiency.

Sincerely,

Tom
