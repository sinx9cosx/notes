You are a team leader and want to invite your subordinate, John, to join a new project.  
  
**Write an email to John. In your email, do the following:**  
  
-Invite John to participate in the project.  
-Explain why you think he would be a valuable member of the team.  
-Ask if he is interested and suggest discussing the project details further.  
  
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Dear John,

I'm writing to invite you to participate in a new project, which works on environmental problems.

I believe you will become a valuable member. First, I admire your ability to search for related information. In previous projects, you always did a good job on that and provided wonderful information for us, which helped us make progress. Besides, you really care about environmental topics. I'm sure you will enjoy working on this project.

Would you be interested in my project? If so, we can discuss the details in our free time. I'm looking forward to your reply.

Yours,

Tom
