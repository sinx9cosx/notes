# 题目

Flu season is approaching, and several students in the dormitories have fallen ill.  
  
**Write an email to Dr. Miller. In your email, do the following:**  
  
-Describe the health concerns among the students in the dormitories.  
-Suggest measures to prevent the spread of illness.  
-Request advice or assistance from the campus health service.  
  
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

# 原文改写

Dear Dr. Miller,
Recently several students in the dormitories have fallen ill. I am worried about their health. They have fevers and headaches, which affect their rest. Without good rest and medical care, it is difficult for them to recover from the flu soon.
I am also concerned about the spread of the flu. Since the students share common areas such as bathrooms and kitchens, the illness could easily pass to others. Therefore, I think separating the sick students from other healthy students is a good choice. Besides, they need medicine and enough water to help them get better.
Could you please give me some advice on how to prevent the spread of the flu and help the students recover quickly? It would also be helpful if the campus health service could provide some masks or hand sanitizer for the dormitories. Thank you very much! I am looking forward to your reply.

Yours,
Tom

# 范文

Dear Dr. Miller,
I am writing to report a growing health concern in the dormitories. Over the past week, at least six students on the third floor of Wilson Hall have come down with fevers, sore throats, and persistent coughs, and two of them have already missed classes. With flu season approaching, I worry the illness will spread quickly through our shared bathrooms and common areas.
To slow the spread, I suggest placing hand sanitizer stations at each dorm entrance and posting reminders about covering coughs and washing hands. We could also ask students with symptoms to wear masks in the hallways and to avoid the dining hall during peak hours.
Could the campus health service provide flu vaccines on site, or send a nurse to check on the affected students? Any guidance you can offer would be greatly appreciated.
Sincerely,
Alex Chen
