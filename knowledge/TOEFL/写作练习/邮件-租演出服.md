## 题目

You are organizing a university theater production and need to rent costumes for the cast. You want to contact a local costume shop to inquire about their rental options, prices, and availability. You also need to know if they offer fitting services for the actors. Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.  
  
**Write an email to Ms. Parker. In your email, do the following:**  
  
-Explain the purpose of the university theater production. -Ask about rental options, prices, and availability for costumes. -Invite them to the production.  
  
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## 回答

Dear Ms. Parker,
I'm preparing a university theater production and need to rent costumes for the cast. We plan to perform Romeo and Juliet, so we need male and female costumes in the medium centuries. Besides, we need 4 pieces of clothes for other characters.
Would you like to tell me if the clothes are available? We will rent clothes for two days on next Thursday and Friday. What options can you offer? Also, I would like to know the prices. If the clothes are good and the prices are in budget, I will rent them without hesitation. In addition, I wonder if you offer fitting services for the actors, because we want to make sure that the clothes are suitable.
The production will be held in our school theater on next Friday, start at 6 p.m. I would be happy if you come! I'm looking forward to your reply.
Yours,
Tom
