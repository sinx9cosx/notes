# 题目

You recently enrolled in an online language course and have enjoyed it. However, you are having some trouble with the online class platform. You need to contact the course instructor, Mrs. White, to resolve this issue.  
  
**Write an email to Mrs. White. In your email, do the following:**  
  
- Mention what you enjoyed most about the course.  
- Describe the issue you are having with the online platform.  
- Propose a solution to resolve this problem.  
  
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

# 回答（4/5）

Dear Mrs. White, 
I enrolled in your English class recently, and I have enjoyed it. The courses are useful to improve my listening and speaking abilities. I have learned a lot, and I can communicate with my school's foreigners smoothly. 
However, I have some trouble with the online platform. I also purchased writing and reading courses, but after I payed for them, I was still not able to study them. 
Could you please check my purchase and inform me? If I payed for them successfully, could you make the access available to me? Thanks for your help. I look forward to your reply.
Sincerely, 
Tom

# 原文改写

Dear Mrs. White,
I enrolled in your English class recently, and I have enjoyed it very much. The courses are useful for improving my listening and speaking abilities. I have learned a lot, and I can now communicate with **the international students** at my school smoothly.
However, I have some trouble with the online platform. I also purchased the writing and reading courses, but after I **paid** for them, I was still not able to study them. **Every time I try to open these courses, the platform shows a message saying that I do not have access.**
Could you please check my purchase and inform me? If I paid for them successfully, could you make the access available to me? Thanks for your help. I look forward to your reply.
Sincerely,
Tom

# 范文

Dear Mrs. White,
I am writing to thank you for the wonderful online language course I recently enrolled in. What I have enjoyed most is your weekly pronunciation workshop, because the slow, repeated drills have finally helped me hear the difference between similar vowel sounds that I had struggled with for years.
Unfortunately, I am having trouble with the class platform. Since last Tuesday, the audio files in the listening section will not play on my laptop. When I click the play button, the page freezes and I have to reload it, which means I cannot complete the listening exercises or review your recorded feedback.
Could you please upload the audio files as downloadable MP3 attachments in the course folder? That way, I could save them to my device and listen offline without relying on the broken player. I would be grateful for your help.
Sincerely,
Alex Morgan
