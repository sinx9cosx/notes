## 题目

Your professor is teaching a class on education. Write a post responding to the professor's question.  
**In your response, you should do the following:**  
· Express and support your opinion.  
  
· Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.  
  
An effective response will contain at least 100 words.

Today we'll discuss the benefits and drawbacks of online learning. Supporters of online learning believe that it offers flexibility and access to a wide range of courses. Critics, on the other hand, believe that online learning lacks the personal interaction and structure of traditional classrooms. In your opinion, which of these factors do you believe plays a larger role in the effectiveness of online learning? Why?

Claire:

I think the flexibility and access to diverse courses would remove a significant barrier that often prevents students from furthering their education. That barrier is location. Online learning enables potential learners to explore specialized fields and skills that might not otherwise be available where they live.

Paul:

I believe that the lack of personal interaction during online learning sessions can be detrimental to true learning. Traditional classrooms provide direct and immediate support from teachers that students don't receive during online learning classes. A classroom teacher can respond to students and answer questions in real time, which is not always possible when taking online classes.

## 原文改写

I agree with Claire's view. I'm interested in math, but I major in chemistry. Therefore, I started to learn analysis online in my free time. I don't need to go to an offline class since my schedule is full, while I can still <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">access the resources</mark>. Another reason is that it's convenient to deal with difficulties online. If you're confused about some concepts, you can just <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">switch to that part</mark> and learn it again without <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">worrying about</mark> being left behind. In offline classes, professors always go ahead, and I often feel frustrated when I fail to catch up.

Admittedly, traditional classrooms offer direct and immediate support from teachers. However, you can also ask the teacher by sending messages, and teachers can also receive feedback and <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">adjust their teaching</mark>. You can't deny that this method is also helpful to both teachers and students. With the development of online class systems, we will have a better experience online.
