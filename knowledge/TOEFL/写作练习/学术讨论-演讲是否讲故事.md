# 题目

Your professor is teaching a class on communications. Write a post responding to the professor's question.  
In your response, you should do the following:  
-Express and support your opinion.  
-Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.  
  
An effective response will contain at least 100 words.  

We've been discussing the role of storytelling in effective communication. Storytelling can be a powerful tool to convey messages and connect with audiences emotionally. Some experts argue that storytelling is essential for engaging presentations, while others believe that factual, straightforward communication is more effective. What is your opinion on the use of storytelling in communication?

Kelly:

Storytelling is essential for successful communication during, for instance, presentations. It helps to connect with the audience on an emotional level, making the message more memorable and impactful. Stories can illustrate points more vividly than plain facts and keep the audience engaged from start to finish.

Paul:

Factual, straightforward communication is more effective. Clear and concise information ensures that the audience understands the message without any ambiguity. While stories can be engaging, they may sometimes distract from the main points. Successful communication is determined more by its ability to convey a message than it is to entertain an audience.

# 原文改写

I concur with Kelly's idea. I think storytelling is essential for successful communication, because it is easy to understand and helps connect with the audience emotionally.

In addition, telling a story in presentations can lighten the atmosphere. When the speaker is not nervous, it is easier for him to continue the speech. For example, when I had a presentation in my English class last year, I was nervous at first. Then I told a story about my experience in high school. The audience just burst out laughing, and I immediately felt relaxed, which helped me finish my presentation smoothly.

As for Paul's opinion, I admit that straightforward communication is good, but the audience may find it hard to understand. Especially for abstract topics in math and physics, people may get sleepy because of the difficult content. So combining facts with the speaker's stories is a great way to keep the audience engaged and make the message clearer.

# 范文

I agree with Kelly that storytelling is essential, but I would add that it works best when it serves facts rather than replaces them. A story gives an audience a reason to care, and once that emotional hook is set, the factual content lands more deeply.
Consider the 2015 "ice bucket challenge" campaign for ALS. The organizers could have simply published statistics about the disease's prevalence and research funding gaps. Instead, they built the message around personal stories of patients and families, which spread through social media and raised over $100 million. The facts about ALS were always present, but the stories carried them to millions of people who would never have opened a medical report.
Paul is right that clarity matters, and a rambling anecdote can bury the main point. The solution is not to abandon stories but to discipline them: a short, relevant narrative that ends by connecting directly to the data. Storytelling is not a substitute for facts; it is the vehicle that delivers them.
