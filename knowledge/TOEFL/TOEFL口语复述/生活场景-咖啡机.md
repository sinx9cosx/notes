# 复述内容

First,fill the water tank with the fresh water.
Place a coffee filter in the slot.
Use the timer to brew before you wake up each morning.
Put your cup on the drip tray before percolation begins.
Select your preferred beverage strength on the control panel.
You can avert disastrous spills and messes by not overfilling the water tank.
For the best possible performance, you will need to clean your coffee machine at least once per month.
