# 题目

You recently moved into a new apartment, but a problem has arisen. Your next-door neighbor often plays loud music late at night, which disrupts your sleep and affects your ability to focus during the day. You need to address this issue to find a resolution.  
  
Write an email to your neighbor. In your email, do the following:  
- Explain who you are and why you're writing  
- Describe the issue of loud music and how it is affecting you  
- Request a possible solution to ensure quiet during late hours

# 我的答案

Dear neighbor,
I moved to this apartment recently and became your new neighbor. I'm writing to discuss a problem about your music and hope we can reach a consensus. 
It seems that you often play music at night, which disrupts my sleep and affects my ability to focus during the day. When I'm about to sleep, the loud music often keeps me awake. Even if I fall asleep reluctantly, I would be woken up by noise. In consequence, I couldn't pay attention to classes in school, which makes it difficult for me to catch up with my teachers. 
Thus, I would greatly appreciate it if you could keep quiet after 10 p.m. Hope you can consider this request and find a possible solution. Thanks for your time! 
Sincerely, 
Tom

# 改进建议

1. **自我介绍要具体**:加公寓号(如 Apartment 3B)和搬入时间,一个细节就能让第一点拿满
2. **语气要友好、非指责**:先表达理解("我明白你可能喜欢听音乐"),再提请求,这是 TOEFL 评分中 "appropriateness of tone" 的加分项
3. **解决方案要具体**:不只说 "keep quiet",给出可操作选项(调低音量、戴耳机、10 点后),更能体现解决问题的能力
4. **写完自查拼写**:TOEFL 拼写错误每个都扣分,交卷前花 30 秒通读一遍,这篇至少能避免 3 处硬伤

# 范文(约 180 词)

```
Dear Neighbor,

I hope this email finds you well. My name is Tom, and I moved into the apartment next door (Apartment 3B) just last week. I'm writing to discuss a small issue and hope we can work it out together.

It seems that loud music is often played from your apartment late at night. Since our bedrooms share a wall, the music keeps me awake well past midnight. As a result, I feel exhausted during the day and find it hard to stay focused in class. I completely understand that you probably enjoy listening to music at night, so I'm not asking you to stop — I'd just like to make a friendly request.

I would greatly appreciate it if you could turn down the volume after 10 p.m., or use headphones during late hours. I believe this small change would help both of us enjoy a quiet and comfortable environment at night.

Thank you so much for your understanding. Please feel free to knock on my door anytime if you'd like to discuss this further.

Sincerely,
Tom
```

范文亮点说明:第一段补全了身份信息(名字+公寓号+搬入时间)并点明目的;第二段先描述影响、再表达共情,避免指责感;第三段给出两个具体可操作的方案;第四段用开放式邀请收尾,礼貌自然。
