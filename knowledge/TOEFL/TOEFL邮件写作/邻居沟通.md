# 题目

You recently moved into a new apartment, but a problem has arisen. Your next-door neighbor often plays loud music late at night, which disrupts your sleep and affects your ability to focus during the day. You need to address this issue to find a resolution.  
  
Write an email to your neighbor. In your email, do the following:  
- Explain who you are and why you're writing  
- Describe the issue of loud music and how it is affecting you  
- Request a possible solution to ensure quiet during late hours

# 我的答案

Dear neighbor,
I moved to this apartment recently and became your new neighbor. I'm writing to discuss a problem about your music and hope we can reach a consensus. 
It seems that you often plays music at night, which disrupts my sleep and affects my ability to focus during the day. When I'm about to sleeping, the loud music often keeps me awke. Even if I fall asleep reluctantly, I would be roused by noise. In consequence, I couldn't pay attention to classes in school, which makes me feel difficult to catch up with teachers. 
Thus, I would greatly appreciate it if you can keep quiet after 10 p.m. , hope you can consider this request and find a possible solution. Thanks for your time! 
Sincerely, 
Tom
