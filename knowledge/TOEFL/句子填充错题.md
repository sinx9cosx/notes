A：Why did you move into a new dorm?  
B：The one I was living in had maintenance issues.
错误：I was living in the one had maintenance issues.
讲解：主干是 The one had maintenance issues；"(that) I was living in" 是修饰 the one 的定语从句（that 作 in 的宾语，可省略）。错误版把两个句子硬拼在一起，导致 had 缺少主语。


A：Why did you miss the team meeting yesterday?  
B：I was studying at the library for my exam.
错误：I was studying for my exam at the library.
讲解：多个状语并列时地点在前、目的在后，应为 studying at the library（地点）+ for my exam（目的）；反序易被误读为"考试在图书馆举行"。


A:What did that student ask about the grading system?
B:He wanted to know if there are penalties for late submissions.
错误：He wanted to know if there are late submissions penalties for.
讲解：正确短语是 penalties for late submissions（"迟交的处罚"），介词短语 for late submissions 作 penalties 的定语，必须紧跟其后。错误版有两处问题：① "late submissions penalties" 把修饰语和中心词顺序颠倒，造成名词堆叠；② 介词 for 被甩到句尾悬空，后面没有宾语，句子不完整。宾语从句主干是 there are penalties，for late submissions 修饰 penalties。


A:What did your tutor say?
B:The technique that my tutor recommended seems to work best for me.
错误：The technique work that my tutor recommended to seems best for me
讲解：错误句主语 "The technique work" 把 technique 与 work 两个名词堆叠，主干不清。正确句 work 还原为动词：主干是 The technique seems to work best for me。"(that) my tutor recommended" 是修饰 the technique 的定语从句，that 作 recommended 的宾语、可省略；学生版 recommended 后的 to 悬空多余——recommend sth to sb 


A:Matthew loved the book you recommended to him.
B:did he tell you what his favorite part was?
错误：did he tell you what was his favorite part
讲解：宾语从句必须用陈述语序（主语在前、谓语在后），从句内不能倒装。what 从句中主语是 his favorite part、谓语是 was，应为 what his favorite part was；学生版 what was his favorite part 把 was 提到主语前，成了疑问语序，在从句中不允许。主句 Did he tell you 是一般疑问句，句首 Did 倒装正确——倒装只发生在主句，从句保持陈述语序。整句：他有没有告诉你他最喜欢的是哪部分？（另：句首 did 应大写为 Did。）
