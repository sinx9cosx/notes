## 题目1：你平时会和朋友或家人去看体育比赛吗？为什么？

Not really. I seldom go to sporting events with my friends or family, mainly because none of us are really into traditional sports like basketball or football. Even if we went to a live match, we wouldn't feel that excited because we barely follow those games. Another reason is that we are busy with schoolwork, so our free time is limited. I sometimes watch a big game on TV, but going to a stadium just doesn't appeal to us.

## 题目2：旅行时，你更喜欢自由探索目的地，还是严格按计划行事？

When I travel, I prefer exploring the destination instead of sticking to a strict plan. I see a little uncertainty as a good thing: if I find an interesting street or café, I can just follow my curiosity. For me, a trip is a chance to relax, and a packed schedule would only make me exhausted. Of course, I still book my hotel and transportation in advance, but I leave my days open. That balance keeps the trip both safe and spontaneous.

## 题目3：你认为人们会倾向于更环保的旅行方式吗？

Yes, I do. I think more and more people will choose eco-friendly ways of travelling. In recent years, people have raised their awareness of environmental protection, especially young people. For example, when I travel with my friends, we usually take public transportation such as the subway or the bus. It is not only greener but also very convenient, since we can get almost anywhere we want. So being eco-friendly and being practical can actually go hand in hand.

## 题目4：你认为旅行是提升独立性和自信心的好机会吗？

I believe travelling is a great chance to boost independence and confidence. When you are away from home, you have to book hotels, make plans and handle problems on your own. Last time I went on a trip with my classmates, I didn't ask my parents for advice like I used to. We solved everything by ourselves, from choosing a restaurant to dealing with a missed train. Small experiences like these make me feel more capable and mature.
