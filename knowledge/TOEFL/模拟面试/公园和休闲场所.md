## 题目1：你能谈谈一次难忘的公园或休闲场所之行吗？

The most memorable visit to a park was with a close friend from junior high school. We hadn't seen each other for a long time, so I invited him to hang out during a vacation. We went to a quiet park, sat on a bench, and talked about our lives and our plans for the future. I felt really understood by him, so I was truly happy. What impressed me most was the peaceful atmosphere. Since there were few people, we could enjoy the moment without the noise of crowded streets, and the scenery was beautiful.

## 题目2：公园和休闲场所为什么重要？（对成年人和儿童分别意味着什么？）

I think parks are important to everyone, but in different ways. For adults, parks offer a relaxing place to unwind after a long day of work. For example, when I finish a tiring day at school, I often go to a park with my family or friends just to slow down and recharge. For children, recreational spaces give them room to run around, make new friends, and practice communicating with others, which is essential for their social development. Being outdoors is also good for their mental health, so I believe parks benefit people of all ages.

## 题目3：有些人认为地方政府应该多花钱建公园和休闲场所，你同意吗？

I definitely agree that local governments should invest more in public parks and recreational facilities. First of all, well-equipped parks encourage people to do outdoor activities. For instance, when I walk through a park and see exercise equipment, I often stop and work out for a while. In this way, parks help people stay physically active, which improves both their physical and mental health. Since a healthy population also reduces pressure on the healthcare system, I believe the money is well spent.

## 题目4：如果土地资源紧张，政府应该优先建住房还是公园？

I think it depends on the specific situation. If a city is facing a serious housing shortage, the government should definitely prioritize building affordable homes, because having a place to live is the most basic need. However, if the housing situation is stable, building parks and recreational spaces would be a smart choice. For one thing, green areas encourage consumption—people tend to shop and dine nearby, which boosts the local economy. For another, parks improve people's well-being, so in the long run, they make the city more attractive to live in.
