## 题目1：你的国家的人下班后通常做什么活动？

In my country, people have many ways to relax after work. The most popular ones are going shopping, having dinner with friends, and doing some exercise. As for me, I love exercising after a long day. When I finish my classes or work, I usually go for a run or play badminton with my friends. It helps me relax my mind and reduce stress. At the same time, it keeps me healthy and gives me more energy. So in my opinion, exercise is the best way to unwind after work.

## 题目2：你需要休息时，喜欢独处还是和别人在一起？为什么？

When I need a break, I definitely prefer spending time with others, especially my close friends. First of all, chatting with them about fun topics helps me relax and forget about my stress. When I feel tired or frustrated, their company always cheers me up. In addition, my friends are a great source of advice. Whenever I have a problem that I can't solve by myself, they are willing to listen and share their ideas. With their encouragement, I can get back to my work with a positive attitude and work more efficiently. That's why I enjoy being with friends when I take a break.

## 题目3：你认为未来的工作方式会发生什么变化？

Well, I believe more and more companies will offer flexible schedules and allow employees to work from home. This trend has advantages for both sides. For employees, a flexible timetable means they can decide when to work, so they can avoid rush hour and spend more time with their families. For example, when people work from home, they can cook their own meals and look after their children more easily. For employers, workers with a better work-life balance are usually happier and more productive. So I think flexible and remote work will become very common in the future.

## 题目4：你是否同意工作与生活平衡很重要？

Yes, I totally agree that a good work-life balance is essential for everyone. If people work all the time without any rest, they will feel stressed and exhausted, which is harmful to both their physical and mental health. On the other hand, when we finish work on time, we can pay more attention to our lives. For example, we can go to the gym to stay healthy, or hang out with our family and friends. These activities help us relax and recharge. As a result, we can return to work with more energy and focus, and work more efficiently. So work should never take over our whole life.
