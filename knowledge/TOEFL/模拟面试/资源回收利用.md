## 题目1：你平时会回收物品吗（如纸、塑料、玻璃）？为什么？

Yes, I do. I regularly recycle paper, mainly because it reduces waste. I often print out notes or write my homework on paper, but I can't always use every page completely. So instead of throwing those pages away, I reuse them — I write my class notes or draft scripts on the back. That way, I make full use of the paper before it goes into the recycling bin.

## 题目2：你是怎么了解垃圾分类和回收知识的？

I've learned about recycling from two main sources. First, my community handed out brochures on garbage classification, so I learned how to sort waste into the right categories, and I try to do that in my daily life. Second, at school we've had lessons on waste recycling and environmental protection, which helped me understand why recycling matters, not just how to do it.

## 题目3：回收时你遇到什么困难？如何克服？

Two challenges come to mind. First, I sometimes get confused about which category a piece of waste belongs to. To deal with that, I use an app on my phone — I just search the item, and it tells me which bin it goes in. Second, it bothers me that some people around me don't care much about recycling. I can't force them to change, so I just focus on doing my own part well and setting a good example.

## 题目4：对于环保意识不强的人，你认为应该怎么办？

I think recycling should be mandatory, not just encouraged. Right now, many people won't sort their waste unless they have to, because there's no real consequence if they don't. If sorting became a rule — with small fines for violations, like in some Chinese cities — people would build the habit much faster. And it's worth enforcing, because every ton of paper we recycle means fewer trees being cut down.
