## 题目2

Describe one memorable experience you have to do for music.Made it special.

For me, it was in my junior high school, at the New Year party. My best friend and I sang "Love Story" together on stage. It was my first time singing in public, so I was a bit nervous. But my friend smiled at me and sang along with me, so I gradually calmed down. After we finished, the whole class stood up and applauded. Since then, I've become much more confident. That's why it's so memorable.

## 题目3

I see. now when you listen to music to yourself or with others.

I prefer listen to music just myself because I can immerse myself into the lyrics and melodies when I'm alone. Country music is my favorite music type and when I finish my long day school work, I would listen to country music. The melody is slow and the atmosphere is quiet. So I can relax my mind. Besides, I would read the lyrics carefully. It's interesting to explore a different life by inmagining the lyrics. That's why I love listening to music myself.

## 题目4

Some people believe that music education should be a part of school curriculum.Do you agree with this opinion?Please explain your reasons.

I agree, because music education is good for children's development in two ways. First, it helps children concentrate. When I was in primary school, our teacher used to play soft piano music while we were reading. I found it much easier to focus on the book.
Second, music teaches children to appreciate art. In our weekly music class, the teacher played everything from classical to pop, and I gradually learned to tell different styles apart and became more sensitive to my emotions. These benefits stay with children for life, so music education should definitely be part of the curriculum.
