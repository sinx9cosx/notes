# 题目1

Thanks for your participation. I'd like to discuss your views on renewable energy. To start, how important is the issue of renewable energy, such as wind and solar power, to you personally? Why would you say you feel that way?

Well, honestly, renewable energy is something I really care about deeply. Like, last semester in my chemistry class, I remember my professor showing us this diagram of carbon emissions over the past fifty years—it was honestly a bit scary. That moment really stuck with me. Instead of just memorizing facts for the exam, I started thinking about what I could actually do. So yeah, I believe solar and wind power aren't just alternatives anymore; they're kind of like a reset button for our planet. Protecting the environment isn't just someone else's job—I feel like it's my responsibility too.

# 题目2

Now, describe a time when you or someone you know tried to reduce energy use. What actions were taken, and what made it easy or difficult?

Um, honestly, I never really thought much about saving energy until my roommate started nagging me about our electricity bill. So now, every time I leave the room, I instinctively reach for the light switch. And I also try to be mindful of water, like turning off the tap while brushing my teeth. But honestly, the hardest part is remembering——sometimes I'm so tired that I just forget. It's kind of like building a new habit; you slip up a lot before it sticks.

# 题目3

Great. In your daily life, have you noticed any renewable energy initiatives in your community? If yes, what have you seen, and how do they impact your area? If not, what's an initiative that might be beneficial for your community?

Um, honestly, the first thing that comes to mind is seeing more and more electric cars around my neighborhood. It used to be rare, but now you can spot charging stations in the parking lot near the supermarket. Actually, my family made the switch too, and driving it feels completely different from our old gas car. It's quieter, smoother, and I don't feel guilty about the emissions anymore like I used to. I think that's the coolest part—it's not just about the environment in general, it's about that small change in your daily routine that starts to feel normal. So yeah, I'd love to see more people give it a try.

# 题目4

Interesting. Many believe transitioning to renewable energy is crucial for environmental sustainability, while others worry about the costs involved. Do you think the benefits of renewable energy outweigh the drawbacks? Why or why not?

Honestly, the upfront price tag of renewable energy used to make me hesitate. You know, building those massive solar farms and wind turbines isn't cheap at first. But here's the thing—technology keeps improving, so the costs have been dropping fast. Nowadays, it's becoming the more affordable option, not just for governments but for regular families too. What really convinces me, though, is the bigger picture. Think about the air we breathe and the planet we're leaving behind. Sticking with fossil fuels feels like digging a deeper hole, whereas switching to clean energy is like planting trees for the next generation. So yeah, the drawbacks are temporary, but the benefits last for decades.
