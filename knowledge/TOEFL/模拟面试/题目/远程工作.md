## 题目1 

Thank you for speaking with me today. Some people prefer working remotely, while others prefer working in an office. Would you be willing to work remotely? Why or why not?

**I'd be happy to work remotely, at least for most of the week.** The biggest draw for me is the flexibility it gives me over my own schedule. **双论点范文**  
  
**I'm firmly on the side of remote work becoming mainstream. Not only does it save people hours of commuting, but it also lets them design a schedule that actually fits their life. When you cut out the daily back-and-forth, you suddenly have time for things you actually care about. And there's another side to this. Companies benefit too. They can hire talent from anywhere instead of being stuck with whoever lives nearby, which means better people and lower office costs at the same time.**  
  
**范文解析**  
  
**1. 回答思路**  
这道题可以从 个人时间自由 和 企业用人优势 两个角度展开。远程办公省去了通勤时间，让员工能按自己的节奏安排生活。同时，公司不再受地理位置限制，可以招聘全球人才，还能省下办公室租金，对双方都有好处。  
  
**2. 话题词汇**  
• save hours of commuting：省下大量通勤时间  
• design a schedule：安排日程  
• cut out the daily back-and-forth：省去每天来回奔波  
• hire talent from anywhere：从各地招聘人才  
• lower office costs：降低办公成本  
• stuck with whoever lives nearby：只能局限于住在附近的人  
  
**3. 衔接表达**  
• 引出理由 1 的位置：Not only does it save people hours of commuting, but it also lets them…  
• 引出理由 2 的位置：And there's another side to this.  
  
**更多补充思路与语料**  
  
**1. 其它回答思路**  
① 心理健康 Mental Health  
远程办公减少了办公室政治和通勤压力，对员工的心理健康有积极作用。  
② 家庭时间 Family Time  
不用通勤意味着能多陪家人，尤其是对有孩子的员工来说意义重大。  
③ 城市去中心化 Urban Decentralization  
远程办公让人们不必挤在大城市，小城市和乡村也能吸引更多居民。  
④ 疫情推动 Pandemic Push  
疫情让很多公司被迫尝试远程办公，结果发现效率并不差，这种经验会延续下去。
