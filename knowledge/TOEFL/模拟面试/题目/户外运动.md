## 题目1：你平时喜欢运动吗？一周通常做些什么运动？

Yes, I love jogging with my friends. After a long day at school, we usually jog in the park near our campus. It's a great way to breathe some fresh air and relax after exhausting schoolwork. Sometimes we jog for about half an hour and then grab a drink together. It refreshes me both physically and mentally.

## 题目2：你更喜欢独自运动还是和朋友一起运动？为什么？

I definitely prefer exercising with friends. Jogging alone feels a little lonely, and I would get bored quickly. But when my friends join me, we chat and laugh along the way, so time passes faster and it's much easier to keep going. My friends also encourage me a lot — on days when I feel lazy, they push me to show up and finish the workout.

## 题目3：你觉得今后人们会进行更多的户外活动吗？

Yes, I think people will definitely do more outdoor activities in the future. Most people now realize that physical health is essential — you can't really enjoy your life without a healthy body. As health awareness keeps rising, more and more people are choosing to spend their free time outdoors, jogging, cycling, or hiking, instead of staying inside.

## 题目4：对孩子来说，户外活动和室内活动哪个更重要？为什么？

I think outdoor activities are more important for children. Playing outdoors pushes kids to communicate and cooperate with others, which builds their communication skills. It's also easier for them to make friends outside, and that is good for their mental health. Besides, running around in the fresh air keeps them physically fit and helps prevent health problems like obesity.
