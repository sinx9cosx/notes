  
How often do you listen to music?

Um, honestly, music is pretty much the background of my entire day. Like, after a long stretch of studying, when my brain feels completely fried, I'll put on some soft piano tracks—it's kind of like a reset button for my mood. But interestingly, I also need music while I'm working. Instead of sitting in silence and getting distracted, having something playing actually keeps me locked in. I guess it just makes even the most boring tasks feel a bit more alive, so yeah, I rarely go a day without it.

  
What type of music do you like best? Why?

Well, honestly, country music has always been my go-to. It's the way the soft, unhurried melodies wrap around stories of everyday life—love, friendship, those small-town moments—that really pulls me in. The lyrics, especially, paint such vivid pictures of countryside living. Instead of just background noise, each song feels like a short story I can step into. I think that's why I keep coming back to it. It's like a familiar porch swing—comfortable, warm, and somehow always exactly what I need.

  
Do you usually listen to music alone or with other people?

Honestly, I'm someone who almost always reaches for headphones instead of speakers. There's something about being alone with a song that lets me sink into it completely. Like, last week during finals, I was staring at my notes without absorbing anything, so I put on some lo-fi beats, and it was like my brain finally exhaled. When there's no one around, I don't just hear the music—I feel it wrapping around my thoughts. So yeah, whether I'm washing dishes or just lying on my bed, listening alone is kind of like pressing a reset button for my mind.

  
Is music important in people's lives?

Well, if you ask me, music definitely plays a huge role in our lives. Honestly, after a stressful day, playing my favorite playlist is like pressing a reset button—it calms me down within minutes and lifts my mood almost instantly. But beyond relaxation, I think music opens doors to emotions we haven't lived through ourselves. Like, when I listen to a song about goodbye, even if I've never been in that exact situation, I can feel the weight and sadness through the lyrics. So yeah, music isn't just background noise; it's a bridge to experiences we might never have.
