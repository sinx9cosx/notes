## 题目 1：Would you be willing to work remotely? Why or why not?

**立场词**：remote ｜ **理由词**：flexibility ｜ **例子主语**：online classes

> I'd be happy to work remotely, at least for part of the week. I haven't had a full-time job yet, but my experience with online classes already showed me how much I value flexibility over my own schedule. When my lectures were recorded last year, I could rewatch the difficult parts and study at my most productive hours — for me, that's early morning. I even had time to run and cook proper meals instead of rushing through the day. That kind of control made me way more productive, not less. So I can definitely see myself working from home in the future.

**细节提示**：补一个具体细节分更高——例如课程名（my statistics lectures）、一个具体时间段（7 to 10 a.m.）。

**可替换衔接**：
- 表态：I'd jump at the chance to… / Remote work appeals to me for a couple of reasons.
- 理由：The biggest draw for me is… / What appeals to me most is the freedom it offers.
- 扣回：That ended up making me far more efficient.

## 题目 2：What kind of remote work schedule would you prefer?

**立场词**：part-time / hybrid ｜ **理由词**：balance ｜ **例子主语**：group project

**学生版范文**

> I'd go with a part-time remote schedule, maybe two or three days a week from home. What draws me to that setup is the balance. I actually learned this from my own class schedule. Last semester, my hardest course moved online while the discussion-based ones stayed in person. On the online days, I had quiet, uninterrupted focus — those were my most productive days. But on the in-person days, I could talk through ideas with classmates and actually build friendships. Honestly, either extreme would wear me out. The mix gives me the best of both worlds.

**细节提示**：给那门课一个名字（my economics course）；给"in-person days"一个画面（debating in the seminar room）。

**可替换衔接**：
- 表态：If I had to pick, I'd say… / My preference would definitely be…
- 理由：What makes it work for me is… / The appeal for me is the balance.
- 扣回：It ended up being the best of both worlds.

## 题目 3：What challenges do you think remote work may have?

**立场词**：communication ｜ **理由词**：slow / misunderstand ｜ **例子主语**：online group project

**学生版范文**

> I think the biggest challenge with remote work is definitely communication. When people aren't in the same room, quick questions turn into long message threads or scheduled calls, and meanings get lost along the way. I've felt this firsthand during an online group project last year. We spent almost three days going back and forth in the group chat just to divide up a simple presentation, because one message got misread and one teammate kept missing updates. If we'd been sitting together, it would've taken ten minutes. So remote work can really slow things down and create misunderstandings.

**细节提示**：给项目内容（a presentation on air pollution）、一个数字（five people in the group）。

**备用思路**（如果题目说 challenges 复数或追问）：blurred work-life boundaries——下班后还在回消息；lack of supervision——在家容易刷手机分心。

**可替换衔接**：
- 表态：The toughest part about remote work, I'd say, is…
- 举例：Take my online group project, for example. / I've seen this happen with…
- 扣回：So things that should take minutes end up taking days.

## 题目 4：Will remote work become a mainstream trend? Why or why not?

**立场词**：yes / mainstream ｜ **理由词**：time saved + global talent ｜ **例子主语**：cousin

**学生版范文（双论点，推荐）**

> I'm convinced remote work will definitely become mainstream, probably in a hybrid form. Not only does it save people hours of commuting, but it also lets them design a schedule that actually fits their life. My cousin is a good example — she used to spend almost three hours a day commuting, but once her company went remote, she used that time to exercise and cook. She told me she'd never go back. And there's another side to this: companies benefit too. They can hire talent from anywhere instead of being limited to whoever lives nearby, and they save a fortune on office rent. With both sides winning, remote work is here to stay.

**细节提示**：表姐可以换成任何一位通勤久的亲戚/邻居；公司可给类型（a tech company / a design firm）。

**可替换衔接**：
- 表态：I'm convinced remote work is here to stay. / Remote work seems like the obvious trend to me.
- 理由 2 引出：And there's another side to this. / But digging a little deeper, companies gain just as much.
