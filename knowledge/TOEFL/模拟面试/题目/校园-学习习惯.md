## 题目4

好的学习习惯对学业成功重不重要/还有什么因素重要

important/persistency/English vacubulary

**示范答案：**

Well, I think good study habits matter <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">more than talent </mark>for academic success.
Some people may say smart students don't need habits, but actually, without a routine, even talented people forget what they learned.
For example, imagine a college student who reviews his notes for just twenty minutes every night. It doesn't sound like much, but after one semester, all the knowledge connects, and he doesn't have to panic before exams. In the end, he remembers things much better than classmates who cram at the last minute.
So I believe small, steady habits are the real key to doing well in school.
