## 一、5 道练习题

**Q1（Agree/Disagree）** Should governments spend more money on renewable energy like solar and wind power?

> I definitely think governments should invest more in renewable energy. First of all, fossil fuels like coal and oil are limited, and burning them causes serious air pollution and climate change. Solar and wind power, on the other hand, are clean and will never run out. For example, China has built huge wind farms and solar plants in the west, which now provide electricity to millions of families. Also, investing in renewable energy creates new jobs. So it's a smart choice for both the environment and the economy.

**Q2（Agree/Disagree）** Should ordinary people be responsible for saving energy, or is it mainly the government's job?

> I believe saving energy is everyone's responsibility, not just the government's. The government can make policies, but it can't control what people do every day. For example, in my family, my mom always turns off the air conditioner when we leave the room, and we use LED lights instead of traditional bulbs. These small habits save a lot of electricity over a year. If every family does little things like this, the total savings will be huge. So individuals and the government should work together.

**Q3（Preference）** Which do you prefer to reduce energy use: driving less or using less air conditioning?

> Well, I'd rather drive less. First of all, I live close to my school, so I can walk or ride a bike, which is actually healthier for me. But in summer, air conditioning is necessary because the weather is extremely hot, and it's hard to study or sleep without it. Also, public transportation in my city is very convenient, and taking the subway saves a lot of energy compared to driving a car. So cutting down on driving is easier and more comfortable for me.

**Q4（Agree/Disagree）** Is it a good idea to require all new buildings to have solar panels?

> Yes, I think it's a great idea. First of all, buildings use a huge amount of electricity for lighting and air conditioning. If they have solar panels on the roof, they can generate part of their own power and reduce their energy bills. For example, some office buildings in Shanghai use solar panels to cover part of their daily electricity needs. Also, if every new building does this, the demand for coal power will drop a lot. It might cost more at first, but it saves money and protects the environment in the long run.

**Q5（开放题）** What is one thing people can do in daily life to save energy?

> I think one simple but effective way is to use public transportation instead of private cars. Cars burn gasoline, which is a limited resource and produces a lot of pollution. For example, if I take the subway to school instead of asking my parents to drive me, I save energy and also avoid traffic jams. In big cities like Beijing, the subway system is fast and cheap, so there's really no excuse. If more people take buses or subways, the air quality will improve a lot.

## 三、高频句型

1. 立场：**I definitely think governments should invest more in renewable energy.**
2. 对比：**Fossil fuels are limited and polluting, while solar and wind power are clean and renewable.**
3. 举例：**For example, some office buildings in Shanghai use solar panels to generate part of their electricity.**
4. 让步：**It might cost more at first, but it saves money in the long run.**
5. 结尾：**So it's a smart choice for both the environment and the economy.**

建议练习方式：先掐表说 Q1 和 Q2（观点题最像考试），说完把录音发来，我按昨天的标准给你反馈——重点还是盯流利度和有没有自我重复。
