## 题目1：科技在你的日常生活中有多重要？How important is technology in your daily life?

Technology is essential to my daily life. I start my day with my smartphone — I check messages from my friends and reply right away, which saves me the trouble of meeting in person when we are both busy. I also do most of my shopping online: last week I bought a birthday gift for my mom in ten minutes without leaving my room, and I had hundreds of options to choose from. Honestly, I can't imagine getting through a day without technology.

## 题目2：使用科技时有没有过沮丧的经历？Have you ever felt frustrated while using technology?

Yes, quite often, actually. The most common problem for me is misunderstanding my friends in online chats. Last month, I asked a friend about her exam, and she simply texted back "fine." Without seeing her face or hearing her tone, I was sure she was angry with me, so I spent the whole evening worrying about it. It turned out she was just busy. Since then, I usually send voice messages instead of plain text, because hearing someone's voice makes their meaning much clearer.

## 题目3：科技是否创造了新的机会？Does technology create new opportunities?

Definitely. As technology develops, it creates jobs that simply didn't exist before. For example, with AI growing so fast, companies like OpenAI and Deepseek are hiring large numbers of engineers and data annotators. My cousin actually got his first job last year as a prompt engineer — a job nobody had even heard of five years ago. So in my view, technology doesn't take opportunities away; it keeps creating new ones.

## 题目4：依赖科技是否会削弱独立思考和解决问题的能力？Does relying on technology reduce our ability to think independently?

I think it depends on how you use it. If you just copy whatever AI gives you, your thinking will definitely get lazy. But if you use it wisely, it can actually train your brain. For example, when I search for information with AI, I always double-check the facts using different sources, and that forces me to compare and judge instead of just accepting answers. So technology itself is not the problem — the user is.
