## 题目1：你平时一周通常去哪些地方？怎么去？

During the week, I usually go to shopping centers with my friends. Since my school is far from downtown, I usually take the subway. It’s convenient, but the trip takes about an hour. We often go there to have a meal or relax after a long day at school.

## 题目2：你的家人和朋友通常怎么通勤？

My parents usually drive to work because it’s more convenient for them. It takes them about an hour to get to their companies. As for my friends, we usually took the subway to school when we were in high school. The trip took about thirty minutes, so it was quite fast. I also think public transportation is environmentally friendly because it helps reduce carbon emissions.

## 题目3：住得离学校或公司近是否提高生活质量？

I definitely think location matters a lot. If you live far from school or work, the commute can be exhausting and time-consuming. As a result, you may feel tired and become less efficient. For example, in my last year of high school, my family moved to a house near my school. I could walk there in only five minutes, so I was able to sleep longer and arrive at school feeling more energetic. That really improved my quality of life.

## 题目4：公司是否应该承担员工的通勤费用？

I don’t think companies should fully cover their employees’ commuting costs. Everyone has a different situation, and some employees may live much farther away than others, so it would be difficult for companies to do this fairly. However, I do think companies should offer a fixed commuting subsidy to help cover part of the cost. This would support employees without placing too much financial pressure on the company.
