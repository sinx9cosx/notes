# 题目

Geography is the study of places, environments, and the relationships between people and their surroundings. Modern geographers use technologies such as geographic information systems (GIS) to collect and analyze spatial data, enabling them to visualize patterns of urban development, resource allocation, and environmental change. This analytical approach **allows** geographers **to** provide **insights** into **pressing** global **issues** **as** climate **change**, **migration**, and sustainable **development**, offering frameworks for informed decision-making and policy implementation. Understanding the geographic dimensions of these challenges is crucial for fostering resilience and adaptability in a rapidly changing world.

# 错题

## pre_ -> pressing

类型：B 词义知道，但语境没想到
原因：空格修饰 global issues，需要形容词并与 issues 搭配；我按 previous 补成 vious，读成 previous global issues（以往的全球问题），但后面列举的是 climate change、migration 这些当前亟待应对的问题。
处理：记 pressing adj. 紧迫的，亟待解决的（pressing issues 紧迫的问题、a pressing need 迫切需求）；press v. 压，敦促 → pressing 作形容词。previous adj. 先前的（previous experience），与 pressing 只差中间几个字母，靠 global issues 的语境选。
