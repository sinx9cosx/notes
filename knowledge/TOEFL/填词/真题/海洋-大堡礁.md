# 题目

> 截图末尾疑似截断。

The Great Barrier Reef, located off the coast of Australia, is the world's largest coral reef system. It **hosts** a **diverse** array of **marine** **life**, including **fish**, mollusks, **and** sea **turtles**. Coral **reefs** are **built** by **colonies** of tiny organisms called coral polyps that secrete calcium carbonate skeletons; these hard structures grow together to form massive reefs over time. The Great Barrier Reef ecosystem is crucial for biodiversity, providing habitats and food for numerous marine species. Climate change, pollution, and overfishing threaten the health of the Great Barrier Reef.

# 错题

## colo_ -> colonies

类型：B 词义知道，但语境没想到
原因：空格在 "are built by ___ of tiny organisms called coral polyps" 中，介词 by 后面需要能被 of 短语修饰的复数名词；我补的是 rful，读成 colorful，句意变成"由多彩的微小生物建造"，与后面 called coral polyps 对不上，正确答案是 colonies（珊瑚群体）。
处理：记 colony n. 群体，集群（生物）→ 复数 colonies（辅音字母 + y → ies）；搭配 coral colonies 珊瑚群体、a colony of ants 一窝蚂蚁；colo- 开头还有 color/colorful，靠 by ... of tiny organisms 的语境区分。
备注：截图红字边界存疑，可能为 col_。
