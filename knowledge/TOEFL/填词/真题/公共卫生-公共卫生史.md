# 题目

Public health is a field that looks at the overfall well-bring of communities, focusing on how society and individual health are connected. Over **time**, public health **programs** have **grown** from **basic** efforts **like** improving **sanitation** to **include** many **strategies** that **deal** with **social** factors affecting health. This history shows the ongoing challenge of deciding how to use limited resources while facing more and more health problems, such as new diseases and long-term conditions linked to lifestyle. As leaders work to solve these issues, they are realizing the importance of teamwork across different fields.

# 错题

## li_ -> like

类型：B 词义知道，但语境没想到
原因：句中是 "have grown from basic efforts ___ improving sanitation"，from ... to ... 结构里这一空引出"改善卫生"之类的例子，需要介词 like
处理： like 作介词表举例：efforts like improving sanitation（像改善卫生这样的努力）；对比 such as 后同样接名词或动名词

## incl_ -> include

类型：B 词义知道，但语境没想到
原因：句中是 have grown from basic efforts like improving sanitation to ___ many strategies，to 后面需要一个动词原形，语义是发展到包含
处理：搭配 grow from A to include B。

## sanit_ -> sanitation

类型：D 不认识单词
原因：sanitation 是 sanitary 的名词形式。
处理：词根 sanit- 表"健康"：sanitation n. 卫生设施，环境卫生
sanitary adj. 卫生的
sanitize v. 给……消毒；搭配
improve sanitation 改善卫生条件。

## strat_ -> strategies

类型：C 没想到这个方向
原因：空格在 many ___ that deal with social factors 中，many 后面需要复数名词，后面 that 从句修饰它
处理：搭配 strategies that deal with ...
strategies for doing sth
同族 strategic adj. 战略的、strategically adv. 战略性地
