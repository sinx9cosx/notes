# 题目

The Industrial Revolution profoundly transformed North America, spurring urban growth through technology and immigration. Many advancements boosted connectivity, accelerating commerce and communication. Yet, urbanization brought **challenges** like **overcrowding**, poor **infrastructure**, and **social** inequality. **Historians** debate **whether** these **changes** fostered **modern** prosperity **or** deepened **divisions** in society. The era's complex legacy continues to prompt scholarly inquiry, revealing how industrialization shaped North America's development and raised enduring questions about progress, equity, and the long-term impact of technological and economic transformation.

# 错题

## infra_ -> infrastructure

类型：D 不认识单词
处理： infrastructure n. 基础设施（不可数）
搭配 poor infrastructure 落后的基础设施、transport infrastructure 交通基础设施。

## Hist_ -> Historians

类型：B 词义知道，但语境没想到
原因：空格在句首作主语，后面谓语是 debate，需要表示"人"的复数名词；我按"历史"补成 orists，拼出的 Historists 不成词，正确答案是 Historians（历史学家）。
处理：记 history n. 历史 → historian n. 历史学家 → 复数 historians（注意不是 historist）；搭配 Historians debate whether ...，同类主语还有 scholars / researchers debate。
