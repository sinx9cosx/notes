# 题目

Archaeological research relies on material evidence to reconstruct past human activity. Pottery shards are among the most informative **artifacts** recovered at archaeological sites, offering insight into **the** daily **lives**, trade **networks**, and **technological** practices of ancient **cultures**. Variations in vessel shape, decoration, and clay composition help **researchers** identify cultural affiliations and date **settlements**. Chemical analysis can trace the **geographical** origin of ceramics, revealing patterns of **exchange** across regions.

# 错题

## artif_ -> artifacts

类型：D 不认识单词
处理： artifacts n. 手工艺品，文物（artifact 的复数）
对比 artificial adj. 人造的（artificial intelligence 人工智能）

## technolog_ -> technological

类型：C 没想到这个方向
处理：记 -logy 结尾的名词变形容词是 -logical
technology → technological、biology → biological、geology → geological、archaeology → archaeological。
