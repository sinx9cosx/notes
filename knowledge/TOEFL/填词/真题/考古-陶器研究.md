# 题目

Archaeological research relies on material evidence to reconstruct past human activity. Pottery shards are among the most informative **artifacts** recovered at archaeological sites, offering insight into **the** **daily** **lives**, trade **networks**, and **technological** practices of ancient **cultures**. Variations in vessel shape, decoration, and clay composition help **researchers** identify cultural affiliations and date **settlements**. Chemical analysis can trace the **geographical** origin of ceramics, revealing patterns of **exchange** across regions.

# 错题

## artif_ -> artifacts

类型：B 词义知道，但语境没想到
原因：`artif` 既是 artifact 的开头也是 artificial 的开头，我按 artificial 补成 artifical；但空格在 "among the most informative ___ recovered at archaeological sites" 中，前面是形容词 informative、后面是 recovered 作后置定语，需要的是复数名词 artifacts。
处理：记 artifacts n. 手工艺品，文物（artifact 的复数）；对比 artificial adj. 人造的（artificial intelligence 人工智能）。informative 后面接名词，先定词性再定词形。

## technolog_ -> technological

类型：C 没想到这个方向
原因：空格在 "and ___ practices" 中，修饰名词 practices 需要形容词；`technolog` 已给出，我按 -ing 补成 technologing，没有往 technology → technological 这条形容词派生上走。
处理：记 -logy 结尾的名词变形容词是 -logical：technology → technological、biology → biological、geology → geological、archaeology → archaeological。
