# 题目

What are some foundational skills for academic success? Critical **thinking** allows **students** to **approach** problems **systematically**, analyze **information**, and **make** connections **between** ideas. **Also** important **are** soft **skills** **like** time management and social skills. Time management is helpful for balancing studies, extracurricular activities, and personal life in an efficient manner. Social skills are good predictors of academic success because those who communicate effectively can form positive relationships with their peers and professors.

# 错题

## systema_ -> systematically

类型：D 不认识单词
原因：空格在 "analyze information ___" 之外的位置上修饰后面的 analyze（原文为 "approach problems ___, analyze information"），需要副词；`systema` 已给出，我没有作答。答案 systematically 是 system 的长派生副词。
处理：记 system n. 系统 → systematic adj. 系统的 → systematically adv. 系统地；形容词以 -ic 结尾时副词加 -ally（systematic → systematically），不是 -icly。
