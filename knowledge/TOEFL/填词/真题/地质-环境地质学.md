# 题目

Earth's physical structure and substances, the processes that act upon them, and the history of the planet and its life forms. Environmental geology **specifically** focuses **on** how **geological** processes **affect** human **activities** and **the** environment. **It** encompasses **areas** such **as** natural **hazard** assessment, resource management, and land-use planning. By understanding the geological factors that influence environmental conditions, geologists can help mitigate the impacts of natural disasters and promote sustainable development.

# 错题

## I_ -> It

类型：C 没想到这个方向
原因：空格后面是谓语 encompasses，空格处需要主语。 It 指代前一句的 environmental geology。

## ar_ -> areas

类型：C 没想到这个方向
原因：空格在 "encompasses ___ such as natural hazard assessment..." 中，such as 引导的列举需要被列举的复数名词
处理：搭配 areas such as ...、in the area of ...
注意与形近词区分：arise v. 出现（arise-arose-arisen）、arias n. 咏叹调。
