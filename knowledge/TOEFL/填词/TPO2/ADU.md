## 题目

Accessory dwelling units (ADUs) offer homeowners flexible ways to use additional space while potentially generating income. These small, secondary residences—often located on the same property as a primary home—can house family members, create rental opportunities, or function as separate workspaces. However, constructing ADUs requires compliance with zoning regulations and building codes. As urban populations grow, ADUs present an innovative strategy for supporting sustainable development through efficient use of urban land and the potential to ease housing shortages.
