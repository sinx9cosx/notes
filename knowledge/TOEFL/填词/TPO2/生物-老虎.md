# 原文

Tigers are solitary animals known for their territorial behavior. Each **male** tiger **establishes** control **over** a **large** territory **spanning** several **square** miles **and** patrols **the** area **regularly** to for prey and maintain dominance.

# 错误

## ma_ -> male

错误类型：A 主动回忆失败

## la_ ->large

错误类型：C没想到这个方向
解决：结合上下文**spanning** several **square** miles

## regu_ -> regularly

错误类型：C 没想到这个方向
