# 题目

Climate change is a long-term alteration in average temperatures and weather patterns across Earth's regions. Researchers **document** warming **oceans**, retreating **glaciers**, shifting **rainfall**, intensifying **storms**, prolonged **droughts**, coastal **flooding**, damaged **reefs**, and **disrupted** harvests **worldwide**. Although some climate variations occur naturally, the rapid warming observed since industrialization is largely caused by human emissions of heat-trapping gases. Reducing these emissions and adapting infrastructure can limit future harm to ecosystems and communities.
