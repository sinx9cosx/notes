# 原文

Sleep is crucial for maintaining overall health and well-being. It **repairs** our **bodies** **and** gives a **chance** for **our** brains **to** process **memories**. Research **shows** that **getting** a full **night** of sleep within 24 hours after learning will help us remember new information better. Lack of sleep not only hurts our learning ability but also affects mood and concentration. For example, we might find it harder to focus, make decisions, and respond quickly.
