## 题目

Formulating a hypothesis is a vital step in academic research, linking observation to theory. It starts with reviewing existing literature to identify gaps or inconsistencies. Researchers then suggest a hypothesis —a tentative explanation or prediction based on observation. This guides study design, determining variables to manipulate and measure. Through rigorous testing and analysis, the hypothesis is confirmed or refuted. This iterative process highlights the evolving nature of knowledge, as empirical data continuously informs and reshapes theoretical frameworks within a discipline.
