# 题目

The enduring legacy of classical civilizations like Rome and Greece is undeniable and continues to influence contemporary society. Their contributions were **not** merely **limited** to **monumental** architecture **or** philosophical **essays**, but **extend** to **governing** systems **and** **cultural** practices **that** persist today. The Roman concept of a republic, for instance, serves as a template for numerous modern political systems. Moreover, Greek philosophical inquiries laid the groundwork for scientific reasoning and ethical debates central to our current educational frameworks.
