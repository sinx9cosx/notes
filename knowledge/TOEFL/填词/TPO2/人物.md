# 题目

James Cook was a British naval officer, explorer, and cartographer born in 1728. He **is** best **known** for **his** three **voyages** across **the** Pacific **Ocean**, during **which** he **meticulously** mapped **coastlines** and **collected** data on astronomy, natural history, and oceanography. Cook is the first European to circumnavigate New Zealand and make contact with the Hawaiian Islands. He is also notable for implementing health measures aboard his ships, such as dietary changes to prevent the disease scurvy.
