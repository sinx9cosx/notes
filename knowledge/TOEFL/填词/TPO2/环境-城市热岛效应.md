## 原文

The urban heat island (UHT) effect is a phenomenon where urban areas experience significantly higher temperatures than surrounding rural regions. This temperature **difference** is **caused** by **the** abundance **of** heat-absorbing **surfaces**, like **concrete**, which **retain** heat **during** the **day** and **release** it slowly at night. Reduced vegetation and limited airflow in cities further intensify the effect, leading to increased energy consumption, elevated air pollution, and health risks, especially during heatwaves.Green spaces in urban areas play a critical role in combatting the effects of UHI.
