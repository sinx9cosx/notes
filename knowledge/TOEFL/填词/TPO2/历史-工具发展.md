# 题目

The development of tools during the time of early hominids was a transformative step in human evolution, reflecting increasing cognitive abilities and adaptability to diverse environments. Beginning with simple stone flakes, tools **were** used **for** self-defense, **cutting** meat, **and** processing **plants**. **As** time **progressed**, more **advanced** tools **like** hand **axes** and scrapers emerged, showing evidence of planning, skill, and possibly even teaching within social groups. These innovations not only improved survival rates but also laid the foundation for cultural evolution and technological advancement.

## 单词

axes
n. 斧子，斧头（axe/ax 的复数）；hand axes 手斧

hominids
n. 人科动物，原始人类（hominid 的复数）

transformative
adj. 变革性的，带来重大改变的

flakes
n. 薄片，石片（flake 的复数）；stone flakes 石片
