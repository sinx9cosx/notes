# 题目

The study of evolution through fossil evidence provides crucial insights into the history of life on Earth. Fossils, **defined** as **the** preserved **remains** of **ancient** organisms, **reveal** how **species** have **adapted** over time. Some fossils **demonstrate** the **gradual** process of **evolution**. By analyzing the morphology and distribution of fossils, scientists can reconstruct the phylogenetic relationships among species and understand the mechanisms driving evolutionary change.
