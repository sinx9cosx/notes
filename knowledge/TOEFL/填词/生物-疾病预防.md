# 题目

Public health is a multidisciplinary field concerned with preventing disease, prolonging life, and promoting health through organized efforts and informed choices. It **involves** strategies such **as** health **education**, vaccination **programs**, and **policies** aimed **at** ensuring **safe** **environments**. Public health **professionals** work to **identify** health disparities. They also manage outbreaks of infectious diseases, promote healthy lifestyles, and contribute to improved quality of life and reduce the burden on healthcare systems worldwide.

# 错题

## in_ -> involves

类型：B 词义知道，语境没想到
处理：两个词在这个语境里语义侧重不同。

- **involves** 强调"需要、牵涉、必然动用"：公共卫生这个领域要开展工作，就必须采用这些策略，强调的是"做这件事需要用到的手段"，带有行动性质。
- **includes** 只表示"包含、囊括"，是静态的列举关系，语气上偏"这些东西只是它的组成清单之一"。
另外记一个用法区别：描述"工作/过程需要用到什么手段"习惯用 involve，描述"集合里含有哪些成员"才用 include 。
## __ -> at

类型：B 词义知道，语境没想到
处理：aim to do sth.  aim at sth.
