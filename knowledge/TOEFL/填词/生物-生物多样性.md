# 题目

Conservation ecology focuses on the preservation and management of biodiversity and natural resources. It **examines** the **repercussions** of human **activities** on **ecosystems**. Conservation **ecologists** investigate **interactions** between **organisms** and their **environments** to **elucidate** their **resilience**. Scientists research is vital in alleviating the impacts of habitat destruction, climate change, and pollution on global biodiversity, thereby ensuring the preservation of ecological integrity.
