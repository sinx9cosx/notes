# 题目

Medieval history, extending from the fall of the Roman Empire to the emergence of the Renaissance, is marked by profound sociopolitical and religious changes. During this **period**, feudalism **became** the **prevailing** socioeconomic **paradigm** structuring **society** into **hierarchies**. It also **witnessed** the **consolidation** of **authority** and the **founding** of universities. Analyzing this era unveils the profound influence of Western civilization, and its legacy continues to inform contemporary institutions, cultural identities, and philosophical thought across the Western world.

# 错题

## prev_ -> prevailing

类型：D 不认识这个单词
处理：prevailing adj.现存的；盛行的 v. 流行

## para_ -> paradigm

类型：D 不认识这个单词
处理：paradigm n. 典范

## hierar_ -> hierarchies

类型：D 不认识这个单词
处理：hierarchy n. 等级制度，阶层，统治集团；层级

## wit_ -> witnessed

类型：C 没有想到这个方向
原因：没有读懂这两句话
处理：翻译：在这一**时期**，封建主义**成为**主导性的**社会经济范式**，将**社会**构建为**等级**体系。它还**见证**了**权威**的**巩固**与大学的**创立**。

## foud_ -> founding

类型：C 没有想到这个方向
原因：没有读懂这两句话
