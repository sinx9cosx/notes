# 题目

The earliest ancestor of the modern bicycle is probably a machine used in England in the early 1800s called a hobbyhorse. It **had** two **wheels** and a **place** to **sit**, but **no** pedals; **riders** would **push** it along with **their** feet. **Later**, an **inventor** added pedals directly to the front wheel, like a modern child's tricycle. This version, with a huge front wheel, was faster, but it was unsafe. Riders were high off the ground, and the two wheels were close together, making both balance and stopping difficult.
