# 题目

Climate change has become one of the most pressing current issues of our time, sparking global conversations. When discussing climate, it is often confused with weather. Climate **specifically** refers **to** long-term **trends** in **temperature**, humidity, **wind**, and **precipitation** that **define** typical **conditions** for a **region**, not **just** the daily forecast. While weather can shift dramatically from one day to the next, the climate of an area describes what is normal over decades.

# 错题

## wi_ -> wind

类型：B 词义知道，语境没想到
处理： wind n. 风（形近词 wild adj. 野生的）
搭配 strong wind 强风、wind power 风能。
