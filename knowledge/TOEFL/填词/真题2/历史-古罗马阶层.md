# 题目

The society of ancient Rome was a complex hierarchy, featuring a distinct class system. At the top were the patricians, the **elite** aristocratic **families** who **held** the **majority** of **the** political **power**. Most **of** the **population** had **limited** privileges, **influence** and rights. They were called plebeians. Despite their larger numbers, plebeians were largely excluded from formal political decision-making in the early Republic. Over time, however, they fought for and gradually secured more privileges, such as the ability to hold public office.

# 错题

## el_ -> elite

类型：B 词义知道，语境没想到
处理： elite n. 精英，上层集团 adj. 精英的
搭配 the ruling elite 统治精英、an elite group 精英群体
