# 题目

The formation of river deltas is a geographical process that illustrates the dynamic interaction between land and water. When a river reaches a body of water, such as an **ocean** or **sea**, it **slows** down, **depositing** the **materials** it has **carried** from **upstream**. Gradually, **the** sediments **accumulate** to **create** deltas, which are fertile landforms that extend into the water. Deltas not only support diverse ecosystems but also provide rich agricultural land.
