# 题目

Terrestrial mammals exhibit a fascinating array of adaptations that help them survive and thrive in diverse environments. From **complex** social **interactions** that **improve** group **survival** to **ingeniously** designed **lungs** that **enhance** oxygen **absorption**, these **creatures** are **built** for resilience. Some have evolved powerful limbs for digging deep burrows, while others have agile bodies perfect for scaling trees or sprinting across open plains. Their sharp senses and stealthy movements are often the result of a high-stakes evolutionary arms race between predator and prey.

# 错题

## com_ -> complex

类型：C 没想到这个方向
处理：搭配 complex social interactions 复杂的社会互动、a complex problem 复杂的问题。

## ingen_ -> ingeniously

类型：D 不认识单词
处理： ingenious adj. 巧妙的，有独创性的 → ingeniously adv. 巧妙地
搭配 ingeniously designed 设计巧妙的、an ingenious solution 巧妙的解决方案。

## lu_ -> lungs

类型：A 主动回忆失败
处理： lung n. 肺 → 复数 lungs
搭配 lung capacity 肺活量、healthy lungs 健康的肺。
