# 原文

Shadow puppetry is an ancient form of storytelling that uses flat, articulated (movable) figures cast onto a screen by a light source. Shadow puppetry <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">combines</mark> visual <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">artistry</mark> with <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">dramatic</mark> narration, creating engaging performances that <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">convey</mark> traditional<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;"> tales</mark> and <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">moral</mark> lessons. This art form originated in Asia and spread to various cultures around the world. The puppets are typically made of leather or paper and manipulated by sticks or strings. Performers narrate stories, often accompanied by music or sound effects.
