The last book you've read? What do you like or dislike about it?

The last book I've read is *Harry Potter*. I love its story and characters. Firstly, it's an exploration story about Harry, Ron and Hermione in the magical world. From the story I was inspired to be brave. Secondly, I love the main characters and I was moved by their frienship.

I think the biggest reason is the story itself — it's this great adventure about Harry, Ron, and Hermione exploring a magical world. And you know, there's this one moment where Harry has to stand up to Voldemort, and it really made me think about what being brave actually means. I also really connected with the characters. I don't think that digital books would totally take palce of printed books. Firstly, printed books are need in school for education. Although digital books are convenient, many children can not use electronic devices. Secondly, many readers still love printed books, turning pages makes people feel more real about books and imerged. So in conclusion, I think printed books won't disappear.
> [!tip] 范文（新版口语化）
> Honestly, I don't think digital books will ever completely replace printed ones. First of all, printed books are still needed in schools — many students are required to use physical textbooks, and honestly, kids already spend way too much time on screens, so I don't see schools going fully digital anytime soon. And there's also the experience part. I mean, a lot of readers, including me, just love the feel of paper — turning actual pages helps you focus, while reading on a screen, there's always a notification waiting to distract you. So yeah, digital books are convenient, but printed books are going to stick around.
