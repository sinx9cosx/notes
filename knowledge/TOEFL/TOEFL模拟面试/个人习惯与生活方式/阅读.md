# question 3
many people prefer reading digital books over printed ones. What's your thought on this trend? Do you think digital books would completely take place of printed books? Why or why not?

I don't think that digital books would totally take palce of printed books. Firstly, printed books are need in school for education. Although digital books are convenient, many children can not use electronic devices. Secondly, many readers still love printed books, turning pages makes people feel more real about books and imerged. So in conclusion, I think printed books won't disappear.
> [!tip] 修改版范文
> I don't think digital books will completely take the place of printed books. First, printed books are still needed in education. Many schools require students to use physical textbooks, and young children shouldn't spend too much time on screens anyway. Second, many readers simply prefer the feel of paper books. Turning real pages helps people concentrate and feel more immersed in the story, while screens can easily distract you with notifications. So I believe printed books will continue to exist, even if digital books become more popular.
