# 常犯语法错误总结

## 中式搭配与正确短语搭配

- buy daily necessities（购买日常必需品）
- necessary facilities（必要的设施）
- widespread use（广泛使用）
- government guidance（政府引导）
- would do better to give（最好给出）
- give 70% of the grade（给出70%的分数）
- inform you of a problem（告知问题）
- keep up with my teachers（跟上老师）
- a satisfactory grade（令人满意的成绩）
- seriously（如 more seriously）
- breathe fresh air（呼吸新鲜空气）

## 冠词与名词单复数

- the government
- the school's new cafe plan
- adapt to a new environment
- exploring a new city
- make the community better
- for the valuable guidance
- space（不可数）
- paper（不可数）
- effort（不可数）
- new types of products

## 介词搭配

- a problem with public facilities
- provided me with your own experience
- deal with it more smoothly
- experience in facing similar problems
- appreciate being considered for the program
- through public service announcements
- on a large scale
- find problems in time
- From my perspective
- the key to attracting students

## 时态

- I am woken up
- I can't pay attention to classes
- will get frustrated
- students who make mistakes
- I would be proud if my products are used
- would be a success
- I hope my recommendations will be considered
- the cafe sell（suggest 后接虚拟语气）
- I partly agree with

## 词形与词性

- I advise you（advise 是动词）
- taught（teach 的过去式）
- self-study
- acknowledging students' efforts
- gain fresh inspiration
- the upcoming internship
- software（不可数）
- breathe（动词，呼吸）

- which saves
- so most students will love them

## 其他典型句法

- there's no need to worry
- considering a new job opportunity（consider 后直接接宾语）
- when they feel thirsty（补出逻辑主语）
- individuals, because they may move（because 从句与主句合并）
- without carefully checking（介词后接动名词）
- what documents I need to submit（间接疑问句用陈述语序）
- which is beneficial for both me and the company（平行结构）
- the most significant concern（最高级后接名词）
- as part of buildings
- drive fashion brands to make environmentally friendly products（drive sb. to do sth.）
