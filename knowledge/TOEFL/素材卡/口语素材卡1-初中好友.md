# 骨架

- **人物**：close friend from junior high
- **事件**：park → bench → he wants to be a doctor, I have no idea about my future
- **时间**：last month
- **感受**：connected and understood

# 短语块

- **have no idea about my future**: I honestly said I still have no idea about my future.
- **feel connected and understood**: That talk made me feel connected and understood, even though we rarely meet now.

# 万能接口句

That's why I value quiet moments with close friends.

适用话题：朋友 / 安静vs热闹 / 放松 / 重要的谈话

# 卡片对应的完整示范回答

Last month, I met my close friend from junior high in the park. We sat on a bench and talked for a long time. He told me he wants to be a doctor, and I honestly said I still have no idea about my future. That talk made me feel connected and understood, even though we rarely meet now. That's why I value quiet moments with close friends.
