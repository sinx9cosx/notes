# 骨架

**人物**: I
**事件**: often print out notes and write homework on paper, but can't always use every page completely; instead of throwing those pages away, I reuse them — write class notes or draft speaking scripts on the back
**时间**: this semester（习惯，可锁定"昨天整理讲义"展开）
**感受**: doing my own part for the environment — makes me feel I'm not wasting resources

# 短语块

- **reuse ... instead of throwing (it) away**: Instead of throwing those pages away, I reuse them.
- **make full use of the paper**: I write my notes on the back to make full use of the paper.

# 万能接口句

"Instead of throwing those pages away, I reuse them."

适用题目：do you recycle / environment / small actions that help the planet / saving resources / good habits

# 卡片对应的完整示范回答

Yes, I recycle paper regularly. I often print out notes or write my homework on paper, but I can't always use every page completely. So instead of throwing those pages away, I reuse them — I write my class notes or draft my speaking scripts on the back. That way, I make full use of the paper before it goes into the recycling bin. It's a small habit, but every little bit helps reduce waste.
