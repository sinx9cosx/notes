# 口语素材卡 8：用 AI 查资料，多来源核实

#flashcards

这张卡的人物、事件、时间、感受分别是什么？
?
**人物**: I（+ AI 工具）
**事件**: when I search for information with AI, I always double-check the facts using different sources; it forces me to compare and judge instead of just accepting answers
**时间**: whenever I use AI for homework（习惯，可锁定为"上周查一个课题"展开）
**感受**: AI actually trains my brain — technology itself is not the problem, the user is

这张卡里两个可以直接进嘴的地道短语块是什么？各造一个句子。
?
- **double-check the facts using different sources**: Whenever I use AI, I double-check the facts using different sources.
- **compare and judge instead of just accepting**: This forces me to compare and judge instead of just accepting answers.

这张卡的万能接口句是什么？（听到哪些题目可以抽它）
?
*"Technology itself is not the problem — the user is."*
适用题目：relying on technology / independent thinking / critical thinking / AI and students / how to use technology wisely

卡片对应的完整示范回答（约 30 秒）是什么？
?
Some people say relying on technology makes our thinking lazy, but I think it depends on how you use it. For example, when I search for information with AI, I always double-check the facts using different sources. That forces me to compare and judge instead of just accepting answers. Used this way, AI actually trains my brain instead of weakening it. So technology itself is not the problem — the user is.
