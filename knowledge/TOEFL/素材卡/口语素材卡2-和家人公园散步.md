# 骨架

**人物**: my family（含 I）
**事件**: took a walk in the park with my family
**时间**: last Sunday evening（比 every night 更好展开——锁定"具体某一次"）
**感受**: slow down and recharge / enjoy the peaceful moment

# 短语块

- **slow down and recharge**: After a busy week, taking a walk with my family helps me slow down and recharge.
- **enjoy the peaceful moment**: We just enjoy the peaceful moment together, without phones or worries.

# 万能接口句

"It's the best way for my family to slow down and recharge after a busy week."

适用题目：family activity / how you relax / weekends / health / balance between study and life

# 卡片对应的完整示范回答

Last Sunday evening, I took a walk in the park with my family. We walked slowly along the lake and talked about our week. There was no phone, no homework — just the peaceful evening. It was the best way for us to slow down and recharge after a busy week. That's why I think simple outdoor activities matter more than expensive entertainment.
