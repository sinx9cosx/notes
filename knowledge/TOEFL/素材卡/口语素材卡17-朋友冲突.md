I'd handle disagreements with friends mainly through honest communication, and if we really can't agree, I respect their choice.

Last semester, my friends and I worked on a group project and our opinions clashed — I wanted to make a poster, but they preferred a slideshow. Instead of insisting on my idea, I listened to everyone's reasons first. After a thorough discussion, we came up with a compromise that combined both ideas. In the end, we finished ahead of schedule and got a great grade.

So I believe open communication can resolve most disagreements. And even when it can't, respecting each other's choices is what keeps a friendship strong.
