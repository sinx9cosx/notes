# 骨架

**人物**: my friends（含 I）
**事件**: worked on a group project and our opinions clashed — I wanted to make a poster, but they preferred a slideshow; instead of insisting on my idea, I listened to everyone's reasons first, and we came up with a compromise that combined both ideas
**时间**: last semester
**感受**: open communication can resolve most disagreements; respecting each other's choices keeps a friendship strong

# 短语块

- **handle disagreements through honest communication**: I'd handle disagreements with friends mainly through honest communication.
- **our opinions clashed**: Last semester, my friends and I worked on a group project and our opinions clashed.
- **instead of insisting on my idea**: Instead of insisting on my idea, I listened to everyone's reasons first.
- **come up with a compromise**: After a thorough discussion, we came up with a compromise that combined both ideas.
- **finish ahead of schedule**: In the end, we finished ahead of schedule and got a great grade.

# 万能接口句

"Open communication can resolve most disagreements, and respecting each other's choices is what keeps a friendship strong."

适用题目：disagreement / conflict with friends / group work / communication / compromise / teamwork

# 卡片对应的完整示范回答

I'd handle disagreements with friends mainly through honest communication. Last semester, my friends and I worked on a group project and our opinions clashed — I wanted to make a poster, but they preferred a slideshow. Instead of insisting on my idea, I listened to everyone's reasons first. After a thorough discussion, we came up with a compromise that combined both ideas. In the end, we finished ahead of schedule and got a great grade. So I believe open communication can resolve most disagreements. And even when it can't, respecting each other's choices is what keeps a friendship strong.
