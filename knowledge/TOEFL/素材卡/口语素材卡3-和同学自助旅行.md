# 骨架
**人物**: my classmates（含 I）
**事件**: went on a trip together; didn't ask my parents for advice like I used to; solved everything by ourselves, from choosing a restaurant to dealing with a missed train
**时间**: last time I went on a trip with my classmates（可锁定为 last summer vacation 展开）
**感受**: felt nervous at first, but proud at the end — more capable and mature

# 短语块

- **handle problems on our own**: We handled everything on our own, even a missed train.
- **boost independence and confidence**: Travelling with my classmates really boosted my independence and confidence.

# 万能接口句

"Small experiences like these make me feel more capable and mature."

适用题目：travel / independence / confidence / learn from experience / growing up / teamwork

# 卡片对应的完整示范回答

Last time I went on a trip with my classmates, I didn't ask my parents for advice like I used to. We solved everything by ourselves, from choosing a restaurant to dealing with a missed train. At first I was a little nervous, but by the end of the trip I felt really proud. Small experiences like these make me feel more capable and mature. That's why I believe travelling is a great chance to boost independence and confidence.
