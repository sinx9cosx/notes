# 骨架

**人物**: I
**事件**: always keep the paper bag instead of throwing it away after buying milk tea; the bags are strong and waterproof, so I reuse them in many ways — carry my lunch box to school, use them as trash bags or to store books
**时间**: every time I buy milk tea（习惯，可锁定"昨天买奶茶"展开）
**感受**: doing my own part for the environment — a small thing that adds up to millions of bags saved

# 短语块

- **keep the paper bag instead of throwing it away**: When I buy milk tea, I always keep the paper bag instead of throwing it away.
- **strong and waterproof**: These bags are actually very strong and waterproof, so I reuse them in many ways.
- **carry my lunch box / use them as trash bags**: I use them to carry my lunch box to school, and sometimes I use them as trash bags or to store my books.
- **protect the environment**: It sounds like a small thing, but if everyone does this, we can save a lot of paper and protect the environment.
- **reuse what we already have**: Of course companies should use less packaging, but before that, we can reuse what we already have.

# 万能接口句

"It sounds like a small thing, but if every customer reuses just one bag, that's millions of bags saved every day."

适用题目：environment / what individuals can do for the environment / saving resources / recycling / young people's environmental awareness / should companies use less packaging

# 卡片对应的完整示范回答

When I buy milk tea, I always keep the paper bag instead of throwing it away. These bags are actually very strong and waterproof, so I reuse them in many ways. For example, I use them to carry my lunch box to school, and sometimes I use them as trash bags or to store my books. My friends do the same thing, and we even compare whose bag design is the prettiest. It sounds like a small thing, but if every customer reuses just one bag, that's millions of bags saved every day.
