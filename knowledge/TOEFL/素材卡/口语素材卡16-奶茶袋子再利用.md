好素材！奶茶袋重复利用这种"个人小事"正好配你之前练的环保、节能、个人责任类题目。给你一套可以直接背的素材：

## 核心故事（约 30 秒版）

> When I buy milk tea, I always keep the paper bag instead of throwing it away. These bags are actually very strong and waterproof, so I reuse them in many ways. For example, I use them to carry my lunch box to school, and sometimes I use them as trash bags or to store my books. My friends do the same thing, and we even compare whose bag design is the prettiest. It sounds like a small thing, but if every customer reuses just one bag, that's millions of bags saved every day.

## 关键词块（卡壳时捡这些说）

- **keep the paper bag instead of throwing it away** 留着袋子不扔
- **strong and waterproof** 结实又防水
- **carry my lunch box** 装饭盒
- **use them as trash bags** 当垃圾袋
- **It sounds like a small thing, but...** 听着是小事，但是……
- **if every customer reuses one bag, that's millions of bags saved** 每人省一个，一天就是几百万个

## 这个素材能套哪些题

| 题目类型 | 怎么套 |
|---|---|
| 个人能为环保做什么 | 直接当主例子 |
| 节约能源/资源 | 省纸 = 少砍树 = 保护森林资源 |
| 年轻人环保意识 | "我和我的同学都这么干" |
| 公司该减少包装吗 | 让步用：Of course companies should use less packaging, but before that, we can reuse what we already have |
| 垃圾分类/回收 | 这是 reduce and reuse，比 recycle 更优先 |

## 30 秒压缩版（备用，题目时间紧时用）

> Actually, I reuse milk tea paper bags in my daily life. After I finish my drink, I keep the bag because it's strong and waterproof. I use it to carry my lunch or store my books. It sounds like a small thing, but if everyone does this, we can save a lot of paper and protect the environment.

小提醒：说的时候注意 **bag（/bæɡ/）** 尾音要收干净，别拖成 "bag-guh"；**waterproof** 重音在前面 WA-ter-proof。这个话题比较生活化，你说起来应该比能源政策题顺——正好练流利度。录音发来我听听。
