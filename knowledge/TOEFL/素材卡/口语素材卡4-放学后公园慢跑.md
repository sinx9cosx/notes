# 口语素材卡 4：放学后公园慢跑

#flashcards

这张卡的人物、事件、时间、感受分别是什么？
?
**人物**: my friends（含 I）
**事件**: jogged in the park near our campus; about half an hour; then grabbed a drink together
**时间**: usually after class（比 usually 更好展开——锁定"具体某一次"，如 last Friday afternoon）
**感受**: refreshed me both physically and mentally; friends push me to show up even when I feel lazy

这张卡里两个可以直接进嘴的地道短语块是什么？各造一个句子。
?
- **breathe some fresh air**: Jogging in the park lets me breathe some fresh air after exhausting schoolwork.
- **grab a drink (together)**: After jogging for about half an hour, we grab a drink together.

这张卡的万能接口句是什么？（听到哪些题目可以抽它）
?
*"It's a great way to breathe some fresh air and relax after exhausting schoolwork."*
适用题目：exercise / free time / how you relax / health / friends / outdoor activities / morning or night

卡片对应的完整示范回答（约 30 秒）是什么？
?
After a long day at school last Friday, I went jogging with my friends in the park near our campus. We jogged for about half an hour, chatting and laughing along the way, and then we grabbed a drink together. It refreshed me both physically and mentally. My friends also keep me going — on days when I feel lazy, they push me to show up. That's why I prefer exercising with friends: it's a great way to breathe some fresh air and relax after exhausting schoolwork.
