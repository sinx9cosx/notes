# 骨架

**人物**: I
**事件**: In high school, I often made things messy and failed to finish my work in time; in college, I started making a weekly plan and now I can finish my work in time
**时间**: high school -> college
**感受**: frustrated and worried → learned a lesson: making plans gives me control over my time and helps me get things done in time

# 短语块

- **make things messy**: In high school, I often made things messy and failed to finish my work in time.
- **fail to finish my work in time**: I failed to finish my work in time, so I felt frustrated and worried.
- **learn a lesson**: I learned a lesson from that experience and decided to change.
- **make a weekly plan**: In college, I started making a weekly plan every Sunday night.
- **break big tasks into small steps**: I break big tasks into small steps so they feel easier to start.
- **finish my work in time**: Now I can finish my work in time and even hand in assignments early.

# 万能接口句

"Planning may take a little extra time, but it gives me control over my time and helps me get everything done in time."

适用题目：personal growth / a lesson you learned / a change in yourself / time management / habits / overcoming a weakness / procrastination / a big change in your life / planning ahead

# 卡片对应的完整示范回答

In high school, I often made things messy — I never kept a schedule, so I sometimes failed to finish my work in time. I felt frustrated and worried. When I entered college, I decided to change: every Sunday night, I wrote a weekly plan and broke big tasks into small steps. Since then, I have finished everything in time and felt much calmer. I learned a lesson: planning gives me control over my time and helps me get things done.
