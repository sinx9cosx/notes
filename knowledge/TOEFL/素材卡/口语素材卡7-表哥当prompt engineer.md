# 骨架

**人物**: my cousin
**事件**: got his first job last year as a prompt engineer — a job nobody had even heard of five years ago; companies like OpenAI and Deepseek are hiring engineers and data annotators in large numbers
**时间**: last year（对照：five years ago 这职业还不存在）
**感受**: amazed and excited — technology keeps creating new opportunities

# 短语块

- **a job nobody had heard of five years ago**: My cousin works as a prompt engineer, a job nobody had heard of five years ago.
- **create jobs that didn't exist before**: AI is creating jobs that simply didn't exist before.

# 万能接口句

"My cousin actually got his first job last year as a prompt engineer."

适用题目：technology creates opportunities / future jobs / AI / new careers / what job will be popular / choose a major

# 卡片对应的完整示范回答

Definitely, technology creates new opportunities. As AI keeps growing fast, companies like OpenAI and Deepseek are hiring lots of engineers and data annotators. My cousin actually got his first job last year as a prompt engineer — a job nobody had even heard of five years ago. So in my view, technology doesn't take opportunities away; it keeps creating new ones. That's why I'm excited about the future job market.
