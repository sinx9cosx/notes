# 万能接口句

"Learning a foreign language makes a real difference — in study, travel, and daily communication."

适用题目：learning a foreign language / travel / communication / tools like translation apps / reading / studying abroad / benefits of learning languages

# 卡片对应的完整示范回答

I believe learning a foreign language is really worth the effort. For my schoolwork, I once read an English scholarly article without a translator, and I could clearly understand the author's meaning — something I could never have done without my English skills. However, last summer, when I went to Japan, I didn't understand Japanese at all, so it was difficult to communicate with local people, and even ordering food became a struggle. Looking back at these two experiences, I clearly see how much of a difference knowing a language can make. So I would say learning a foreign language makes a real difference — in study, travel, and daily communication.
