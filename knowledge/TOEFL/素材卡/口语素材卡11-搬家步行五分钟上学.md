# 骨架

**人物**：my family（含 I）
**事件**: moved to a house near my school in my last year of high school; could walk there in only five minutes; slept longer and arrived at school feeling more energetic
**时间**: in my last year of high school
**感受**: really improved my quality of life — location matters a lot

# 短语块

- **within walking distance (of)**: Our new house was within walking distance of my school.
- **improve my quality of life**: Moving closer to school really improved my quality of life.

# 万能接口句

"I could walk there in only five minutes, so I was able to sleep longer and feel more energetic."

适用题目：where to live / commute / quality of life / time management / an important decision / live close to school or not

# 卡片对应的完整示范回答

I definitely think location matters a lot. In my last year of high school, my family moved to a house near my school. I could walk there in only five minutes, so I was able to sleep longer and arrive at school feeling more energetic. Before that, I used to spend a lot of time on the road and always felt tired. That move really improved my quality of life. So in my opinion, living close to school or work is always worth it.
