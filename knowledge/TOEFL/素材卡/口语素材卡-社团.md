# 骨架

- **人物**：I
- **事件**：join the running club->jog with club members in the school twice a week
- **时间**：since I went to college
- **感受**：improve physical health, enjoy exercising, relax after a long day of school work

# 短语块

- **improve my physical health**: Jogging with club members twice a week has really improved my physical health.
- **relax after a long day**: Running with the group helps me relax after a long day of schoolwork, especially when I'm stressed about assignments.

# 万能接口句

"Joining a club is a great way to stay active and meet like-minded people."

适用题目：clubs and activities / exercise / how you relax / making friends / campus life / healthy habits

# 卡片对应的完整示范回答

Since I went to college, I joined the running club and jog with club members twice a week on campus. We usually meet in the late afternoon, run a few laps around the track, and stretch together. It has really improved my physical health, and I now enjoy exercising more than before. More importantly, it helps me relax after a long day of schoolwork — when I run with the group, I can forget about my assignments for a while. That's why I think joining a club is a great way to stay active and meet like-minded people.
