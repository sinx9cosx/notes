# 口语素材卡 5：十分钟网购生日礼物

#flashcards

这张卡的人物、事件、时间、感受分别是什么？
?
**人物**: my mom（含 I）
**事件**: bought a birthday gift for my mom online — compared prices, picked one she would like, without leaving my room; had hundreds of options to choose from
**时间**: last week
**感受**: so convenient — can't imagine getting through a day without technology

这张卡里两个可以直接进嘴的地道短语块是什么？各造一个句子。
?
- **do (most of) my shopping online**: I do most of my shopping online because it saves me a lot of time.
- **have hundreds of options to choose from**: Online shops give me hundreds of options to choose from.

这张卡的万能接口句是什么？（听到哪些题目可以抽它）
?
*"Last week I bought a birthday gift for my mom in ten minutes without leaving my room."*
适用题目：technology in daily life / online shopping / gifts / convenience / save time / do you like cooking...（任何"科技带来便利"题）

卡片对应的完整示范回答（约 30 秒）是什么？
?
Technology is essential to my daily life. Last week I bought a birthday gift for my mom in ten minutes without even leaving my room. I just opened a shopping app, compared the prices, and picked the one she would like — there were hundreds of options to choose from. Honestly, I can't imagine getting through a day without technology. That's why I think online shopping is one of the most convenient things technology has brought us.
