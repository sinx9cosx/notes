# 骨架

**人物**: I
**事件**: trained for a 10-kilometer race; set a goal of running every morning at 6 a.m.; put my alarm across the room so I had to get up; stuck with it for a month and finished the race
**时间**: last year
**感受**: hard, especially on cold days — but a real sense of achievement; taught me how to be persistent

# 短语块

- **stick with it**: It was hard, but I stuck with it for a whole month.
- **a (real) sense of achievement**: Finishing the race gave me a real sense of achievement.

# 万能接口句

"That experience taught me how to be persistent."

适用题目：set goals / overcome difficulties / self-discipline / sports / a sense of achievement / healthy habits / morning person

# 卡片对应的完整示范回答

Yes, I set goals regularly, because clear goals give me direction and keep me self-disciplined. For example, last year I trained for a 10-kilometer race. I set a goal of running every morning at 6 a.m., and I put my alarm across the room so I had to get up. It was hard, especially on cold days, but I stuck with it for a month and finally finished the race. That experience taught me how to be persistent. So setting goals is essential for me — not just for fitness, but for building self-discipline.
