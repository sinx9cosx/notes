# 骨架

- **人物**：I
- **事件**：use a budgeting app to keep track of what I spend every month, the AI in the app analyzes my records and helps me make future plans
- **时间**：since I went to college
- **感受**：feel clear about my consumption habits, raise my awareness of managing money

# 短语块

- **keep track of what I spend**: The budgeting app helps me keep track of what I spend every month.
- **raise my awareness of managing money**: Keeping a monthly record has really raised my awareness of managing money.

# 万能接口句

"Managing money is a habit everyone should build in college."

适用题目：money management / a useful app / habits / technology helps / personal growth / saving money

# 卡片对应的完整示范回答

Since I went to college, I have been using a budgeting app to keep track of what I spend every month. Whenever I buy something, I enter it into the app, and the AI in the app analyzes my records and helps me make future plans. Thanks to that, I now feel clear about my consumption habits, and I have raised my awareness of managing money. I no longer spend money without thinking, and I even started saving a little each month. That's why I believe managing money is a habit everyone should build in college.
