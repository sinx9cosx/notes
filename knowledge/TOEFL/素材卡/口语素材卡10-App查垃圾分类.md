# 口语素材卡 10：用 App 查垃圾分类

#flashcards

这张卡的人物、事件、时间、感受分别是什么？
?
**人物**: I（+ 手机 App）
**事件**: sometimes get confused about which category a piece of waste belongs to; open an app, search the item, and it tells me which bin it goes in
**时间**: whenever I sort waste at home（可锁定"昨天扔一杯奶茶"展开）
**感受**: no longer confused — a little technology makes recycling much easier

这张卡里两个可以直接进嘴的地道短语块是什么？各造一个句子。
?
- **get confused about which category ... belongs to**: I sometimes get confused about which category a piece of waste belongs to.
- **search the item on an app**: I just search the item on an app, and it tells me which bin it goes in.

这张卡的万能接口句是什么？（听到哪些题目可以抽它）
?
*"I just search the item on my phone, and it tells me which bin it goes in."*
适用题目：difficulties of recycling / environment / how technology helps / solve problems / green lifestyle

卡片对应的完整示范回答（约 30 秒）是什么？
?
Recycling sounds easy, but I do face one challenge: I sometimes get confused about which category a piece of waste belongs to. For example, yesterday I wasn't sure which bin a milk tea cup should go in. To deal with that, I use an app on my phone — I just search the item, and it tells me which bin it goes in. With a little help from technology, recycling becomes much easier. So when there's a will, there's a way.
