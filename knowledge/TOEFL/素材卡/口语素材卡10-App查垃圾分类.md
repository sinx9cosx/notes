**感受**: no longer confused — a little technology makes recycling much easier

# 万能接口句

适用题目：difficulties of recycling / environment / how technology helps / solve problems / green lifestyle

# 卡片对应的完整示范回答

Recycling sounds easy, but I do face one challenge: I sometimes get confused about which category a piece of waste belongs to. For example, yesterday I wasn't sure which bin a milk tea cup should go in. To deal with that, I use an app on my phone — I just search the item, and it tells me which bin it goes in. With a little help from technology, recycling becomes much easier. So when there's a will, there's a way.
