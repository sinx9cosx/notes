# 题目

Your professor is teaching a class. Write a post responding to the professor's question.  
· Express and support your personal opinion.  
· Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.  
An effective response will contain at least 100 words.

Doctor Gupta

We're studying the benefits of different approaches to protecting wildlife and natural environments. Some experts argue that creating parks and wildlife reserves where no human development is allowed is the most effective way to protect animals and ecosystems. Others believe that teaching farmers to reduce pesticide use and preserve soil quality is more important because agriculture affects much larger land areas where both wildlife and food production must coexist. Which approach do you think produces better environmental protection and why?

Claire

Parks and wildlife preserves offer better environmental protection results because endangered species can reproduce there without human interference. When large areas of forests, wetlands, and grasslands remain completely undeveloped, they maintain natural water cycles, prevent soil erosion, and preserve complex food webs that support hundreds of different plant and animal species that cannot survive in agricultural or urban environments.

Paul

Ecologically-friendly agriculture is better for the environment because farmland covers much larger areas than parks and wildlife preserves. When farmers reduce harmful pesticides and plant trees along field borders, they create wildlife habitats and cleaner water systems. These farming practices also allow continued food production while protecting the environment, which is more realistic than converting all land to wilderness areas.

# 回答

I totally agree with Paul's idea. I think preserve environment and reduce pesticide use is more important, because it has an influence in a large scale. The polluted food not only influence animals but also do bad on human since human would eat some kinds of these animals.  
If people have eco-friendly agriculture, the soil will be good and water systems will be clean. It's beneficial for wildlife to live in a better place, and the numbers of endangered species will naturally grow up.  
As for building parks and wildlife preserves, it's not as efficient as ecofriendly agriculture. Some species are so endangered that they need human help to survive and reproduce. But if the environment can't be better, they will still face the problems of habitats loss and food shortage.  
In conclusion, I think developing eco-friendly agriculture produces better environmental protection.

# 范文

I agree more with Paul that environmentally friendly agriculture is a more practical approach to protecting wildlife and natural environments. Although wildlife reserves can provide safe habitats for endangered species, they only protect the areas that are specifically designated for conservation. Agriculture, on the other hand, covers huge areas of land and has a direct impact on ecosystems. If farmers reduce pesticide use and plant trees around their fields, they can protect wildlife habitats and improve soil and water quality while continuing to produce food.  
 That said, I understand Claire's point that protected areas can preserve ecosystems with minimal human interference. However, completely separating wildlife from human activities is not always realistic, especially as the global population continues to grow. In my opinion, encouraging farmers to adopt sustainable practices can create a better balance between environmental protection and human needs. This approach allows people to produce food while also reducing the damage that agriculture causes to local ecosystems.
