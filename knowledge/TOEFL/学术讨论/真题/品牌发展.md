# 题目

Your professor is teaching a class on marketing. Write a post responding to the professor's question.

· Express and support your opinion.

· Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.

An effective response will contain at least 100 words.

Doctor Gupta

Next week, we will be discussing the impact of brand loyalty on consumer behavior. Brand loyalty refers to consumers consistently purchasing products from the same brand due to positive experiences and trust. Some marketers argue that building brand loyalty is essential for long-term success, while others believe that attracting new customers should be the priority. What do you think is more important for a company, building brand loyalty or attracting new customers?

Claire

Building brand loyalty is more important for long-term success. Loyal customers provide consistent revenue and often become brand advocates, attracting new customers through word-of-mouth recommendations.

Paul

Attracting new customers should be the priority. Expanding the customer base can lead to increased market share and growth opportunities. Focusing on new customers can also diversify the revenue streams for the company.

# 回答

I concur with Claire's idea that buiding brand loyalty is more important. because they provide consistent revenue and attract new customers as well.  
In addition, it's easier to attract old customers to buy than new customers. The customers who bought a brand before with positive experience will tend to buy the same brand next time, because they think it's familiar. Also it can reduce the time cost for seeking new products, making shopping more efficiently.  
Although it's also important to expand the customers, it's not the most important thing to do. If the brand just focus on new customers and fail to maintain old customers, they will lose trust on the brand, which is bad for future development.  
In conclusion, prioritizing buiding the brand loyalty is more efficient to reach success.

# 范文

I believe building brand loyalty is more important for a company's long-term success because loyal customers make a business less vulnerable to price competition. When customers trust a brand and are familiar with its products, they are less likely to switch to a competitor simply because another company offers a slightly lower price. For example, a customer who has had consistently good experiences with a particular coffee brand may continue buying from it even when another cafe offers a cheaper drink. This allows the company to maintain more stable sales without constantly lowering its prices to compete. Attracting new customers is certainly important, especially when a company is entering a new market. However, acquiring customers can be expensive because companies have to spend money on advertising, promotions, and discounts. If those customers leave after making only one or two purchases, the company may not recover the cost of attracting them. Therefore, I think companies should prioritize building lasting relationships with existing customers, since strong loyalty can provide both financial stability and greater resistance to competitors.
