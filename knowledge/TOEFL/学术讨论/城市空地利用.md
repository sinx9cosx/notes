# 题目

Your professor is teaching a class on public administration. Write a post responding to the professor's question. In your response, you should do the following.  
• Express and support your opinion.  
• Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.  
An effective response will contain at least 100 words.

In our textbook, we read that parks and other green spaces with trees and plants have numerous benefits for people in cities. But city governments sometimes must choose between using a piece of available land as a green space or using it for other important purposes, such as for shopping areas or for manufacturing plants. In your opinion, which is the better use for available urban space? Why?

Kelly:

It’s true that parks can benefit people in cities, especially health-wise. But it’s more important to use the limited space for things that people cannot survive without, like hospitals or clinics. Medical care is often an urgent necessity. Visiting a park is not.

Claire:

I’m all for creating green spaces for people to enjoy, but you don’t necessarily need an open piece of land to do that. Let the available land be used for business or housing purposes. Green spaces can be created on the rooftops of existing buildings. And new buildings can be constructed creatively to include open spaces with plants and trees.

# 我的回答

I concur with Kelly's opinion. It's more important to use limited spaces for necessary constructions, instead of parks for relaxation. Besides, just like Claire says, green spaces can be created on the rooftops or with buildings, which save spaces for other buildings and also refresh air.
In my opinion, government can use a piece of available land depending on the specific situation of the nearby area. For example, if there are many houses and apartments, it's good to build a shopping center, which is convenient for people to find living products. On the other hand, if the area alredy offers many convenient services, such as a hospital, a market and so on, then it's a great choice to build a park for people to relax and exercise. People will breath refresh air and communicate with others in the park, which can enhance their physical and mental health.
In conclusion, the way of using available spaces depends on various situations.
