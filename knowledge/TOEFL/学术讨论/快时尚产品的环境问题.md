# 题目

our professor is teaching a class on economics. Write a post responding to the professor's question.  
**In your response, you should do the following:**  
· Express and support your opinion.  
  
· Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.  
  
An effective response will contain at least 100 words.

Fast fashion refers to the rapid production of inexpensive clothing for mass-market sales. Fast fashion allows consumers to buy trendy items at low prices and provides job opportunities in manufacturing countries. But critics argue that fast fashion leads to vast amounts of pollution in the form of discarded clothing, which harms the environment, and that workers experience poor working conditions. In your view, what would be the most effective solution to the problems associated with fast fashion?

Andrew:

I believe that laws should be created to hold fashion brands accountable for the problems their products create. This responsibility could push them to innovate and create new, environmentally friendly production methods. These innovations might not only reduce environmental damage but also improve conditions for workers.

Kelly:

Based on my experience, today’s consumers demand products that are made in environmentally and socially responsible ways. As a result, market forces are effective at getting companies to adopt better practices voluntarily. Consumer demand can encourage positive change without the need for strict accountability being imposed on clothing makers.

# 回答

I agree with Andrew's view that laws should be created to drive fashion brands making environmentally friendly products. In addition, government should encourage people to buy those products by public service announcement and shopping <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">subsidy</mark>. It's useful for people to raise the awareness of environmental protection. Especially these days, the pollution really affects living conditions and it's bad for workers' health. So using low price to encourage people to shop is a good starting point. For example, if costumers can buy green products which benefits from subsidies at a low price, then fewer people would buy more expensive ones, which would<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;"> restrain</mark> more non-green products. Therefore, I think the guide from government is a nessecary point.

# 评审意见

> [!info] 总体评估
> **预估得分：3 分（官方 0–5 整分制），区间约 2.5–3.5 分，达到 4 分的可能性较低。**
> 1. **观点明确并展开**：观点可辨（立法 + 政府补贴），但"立法"这条线只点了题，全文实际只围绕补贴展开，存在观点漂移，展开不足。
> 2. **对讨论的贡献（高分关键）**：只重复性同意了 Andrew，完全没有回应 Kelly——而 Kelly 的"市场力量已足够、无需立法"恰好直接攻击作者"政府干预"的立场，这是本文上不了 4 分的最大原因。
> 3. **词数**：原文 116 词，满足 100 词下限。
> 4. **语言准确**：拼写错误 2 处（costumers、nessecary）、主谓一致错误 1 处（which benefits）、冠词与可数名词裸用多处、搭配生硬多处（restrain products、guide from government 等），错误密度偏高，属积累性扣分。

## 逐句评审

### 第 1 句
> I agree with Andrew's view that laws should be created to drive fashion brands making environmentally friendly products.

- **语法错误**：drive 不存在"宾语 + 现在分词"的复合宾语结构，应为 drive sb. to do sth.（或 into doing sth.）。
- **修改**：I agree with Andrew that laws should be enacted to drive fashion brands to produce environmentally friendly products.

### 第 2 句
> In addition, government should encourage people to buy those products by public service announcement and shopping subsidy.

- **建议**：government 泛指时加 the（TOEFL 稳妥写法；泛指裸用在英美英语中有实证，不算硬性语法错误）。
- **语法错误**：public service announcement 与 shopping subsidy 两个单数可数名词裸用，需加冠词或变复数。
- **地道性**：by + 光杆名词表手段限于 by car/mail 等惯用搭配，此处 through 更自然；shopping subsidy 生硬，宜用 purchase subsidies。
- **修改**：In addition, the government should encourage consumers to purchase such products through public service announcements and purchase subsidies.

### 第 3 句
> It's useful for people to raise the awareness of environmental protection.

- **语义错位（全篇最值得注意的一处）**：字面意思是"人们提高环保意识这件事是有用的"，而作者想说的是"（政府措施）有助于提高人们的环保意识"，逻辑主语弄反了。
- **语法瑕疵**：raise awareness of... 中定冠词多余。
- **修改**：These measures would help raise public awareness of environmental protection.

### 第 4 句
> Especially these days, the pollution really affects living conditions and it's bad for workers' health.

- **语法错误**：the pollution 定冠词误用（泛指不可数名词不加 the）。
- **用词不当**：really 过于口语化，宜用 severely。
- **衔接问题**：前句谈"怎么做"，此句突然谈"现状多糟"，缺一句过渡；workers' health 突兀引入、与服装业工作条件无显式连接。
- **修改**：This is especially urgent today, because pollution severely affects living conditions and poses serious risks to workers' health.

### 第 5 句
> So using low price to encourage people to shop is a good starting point.

- **语法错误**：low price 单数可数名词裸用，应为 low prices。
- **用词问题**：encourage people to shop 缺宾语限定（应点明是绿色产品）。
- **风格**：So 句首偏口语化（学术讨论属网络讨论语体，容忍度较高，非扣分点）。
- **逻辑跳跃（本句最大问题）**：从"污染严重"直接推出"低价是好起点"，中间缺"消费者对价格敏感、绿色产品因成本高卖不动"这个前提。
- **修改**：Therefore, lowering the price of green products through subsidies is a good starting point.
- **逻辑桥梁（可插在句前）**：Since most consumers still choose clothes based on price, making green fashion cheaper is the most direct way to change their behavior.

### 第 6 句
> For example, if costumers can buy green products which benefits from subsidies at a low price, then fewer people would buy more expensive ones, which would restrain more non-green products.

- **拼写错误**：costumers（戏服供应商）→ consumers，属高风险拼写错误。
- **主谓一致错误**：which benefits 先行词是 green products（复数），应为 benefit。
- **语义不当**：products which benefit from subsidies 逻辑主语错位——受益的是消费者或价格，不是产品本身；宜说 subsidized green products。
- **用词不当**：restrain 的宾语通常是人、情绪、行为或增长类名词，restrain products 不成立，应为 curb the production of 或 discourage people from buying。
- **润色建议**：more expensive ones 建议点明 their non-green counterparts（先行词无实质歧义，属润色而非错误）；近 30 词长句含两个定语从句，建议拆句。
- **冗余提示**：本句与第 5 句实质是同一个点（低价 → 消费者转向）说了两遍。
- **修改**：For example, if consumers can buy subsidized green clothing at a lower price, fewer of them would choose expensive non-green items. This shift in demand would curb the production of environmentally harmful garments.

### 第 7 句
> Therefore, I think the guide from government is a nessecary point.

- **拼写错误**：nessecary → necessary。
- **用词不当**：guide 作名词意为"指南手册/向导"，"政府引导"应为 government guidance；a necessary point 空泛无力，宜为 a necessary measure 或 is essential。
- **结构问题**：口号式收尾，既未回应 Kelly，也未收拢"环境 + 工人"两个问题。
- **修改**：Therefore, government guidance, backed by legislation, is a necessary measure to solve the problems caused by fast fashion.

## 结构逻辑评估

1. **论证链条断裂**：完整链条应为"立法约束品牌 + 补贴降低绿色产品价格 → 消费者转向绿色产品 → 抑制非绿色产品生产"，作者只写了中后段，"立法"完全悬空，第 4 句到第 5 句缺"消费者价格敏感"的前提，第 3 句（提高意识）与第 5 句（价格手段）之间也缺衔接。
2. **与同学观点互动不足（最大短板）**：对 Andrew 仅是重复性同意；对 Kelly 零回应。作者本可以用"绿色产品成本高、定价贵，自愿改进的公司会因价格劣势流失顾客，所以市场力量不足"来有力反驳，全文只字未提。
3. **例子有效性中等**：第 6 句是 if...then... 假设推演，非具体实例；且与第 5 句冗余。
4. **扣题小瑕疵**：全文未出现 fast fashion 一词，可回扣题目关键词。

> [!tip] 改进要点（按优先级）
> 1. **学会回应反方观点（最高优先级，直接决定能否上 4 分）**：Kelly 必须被回应。练习模板：先概括对方观点（Kelly argues that...）→ 指出漏洞（However, ...）→ 一句因果论证支撑（because...）。
> 2. **消灭基础语言错误**：拼写（consumers、necessary 这类高频词）、主谓一致、冠词与可数名词裸用；建议建立个人易错清单，写完留 30 秒只查这三类。
> 3. **提升用词地道性**：restrain products → <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">curb（抑制） the production of</mark>；guide from government → government guidance；really → severely；drive sb. doing → drive sb. to do。
> 4. **补足逻辑桥梁**：每写一个 So / Therefore 之前，检查它的前提是否已明写出来。
> 5. **把"同意"变成"贡献"**：同意他人观点时必须追加新论据或新角度（如立法的具体机制：extended producer responsibility、罚款、绿色税收），而不是重复对方已说的内容。

# 范文

I agree with Andrew that legislation is the most effective answer, but I find Kelly's optimism about market forces unconvincing. Consumer demand for sustainable fashion, while growing, is still too weak to drive industry-wide change. Most shoppers care more about price than ethics, so companies that voluntarily raise wages or use eco-friendly materials risk losing customers to cheaper rivals. Binding laws, by contrast, force every brand to internalize the environmental and social costs of production, creating a level playing field. Governments should, however, make compliance easier by combining regulation with incentives, such as subsidizing green products and taxing wasteful production. When sustainable clothing becomes the cheaper choice, consumers will follow, and brands will gain a genuine motive to innovate. Only this combination of legal pressure and market pull can curb fast fashion's harm to both workers and the environment.

**范文说明**：
1. 首句即概括 Kelly 立场并表态反驳，随后给出两条理由（消费者重价格轻伦理；自愿改进的公司会输给低价对手）。
2. 对 Andrew 不止说"同意"，还补上新论据（internalize costs 内化成本、level playing field 公平竞争）。
3. 立场升级为"立法为主、补贴配套"——既坚持 Andrew 的立法主张，又把学生原文的补贴思路收编为配套工具，主题不漂移。
4. 句式有变化：让步插入语（while growing）、分词结构（creating a level playing field）、长短句交错。
5. 结尾收拢"工人 + 环境"两个问题，呼应题目。
