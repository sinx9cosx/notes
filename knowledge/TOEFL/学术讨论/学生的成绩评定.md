# 题目

Your professor is teaching a class on education. Write a post responding to the professors question. In your response, you should do the following.
• Express and support your opinion.  
• Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.  
An effective response will contain at least 100 words.

This week，I want you to discuss assessment criteria. Some schoolteachers give high grades (marks) only to those students who perform well on their homework assignments and tests. Other teachers, however, may give high grades to students who have worked very hard on their assignments but have not performed so well. Which approach do you think is better: Giving grades based only on performance, or grading students not just on performance but also on their effort?

Kelly:

I think grades should be based on performance only. It’s a matter of fairness. I’d be really upset if another student got nearly the same grade that l did when l turned in work that was almost perfect and the other student’s work had incorrect answers or unclear ideas. It wouldn’t matter to me if they worked harder.

Paul:

Sure, evaluation should mainly be based upon the student’s performance, but hard work should count for something. A student who works hard, even when the final grade isn’t the highest in the class, builds character, which will benefit the student later in life when they go to university or apply for a job.

# 回答

For my perspective, I agree with Paul's view. Because admitting students' efforts is good for students' confidence and ethusiasm for learning. But if teachers only judge on their performance and ignore their efforts, students would get frustrated easily. Because not everyone is good at study, it's teachers' duty to discover different students' potential. Obviously, teachers would better give grades to students depending on both performance and efforts. I think that teachers can serve 70% grade for performance and 30% grade for efforts, which is fair for everyone to get a reasonable grade. In this case, students with perfect performance will get satisfying grade, while students who had incorrect answers would be encouraged and persistent to work hard.

# 评审意见

> [!info] 总体评估
> **预估得分：3 分（官方 0–5 整分制），区间约 2.5–3.5。**
> 1. **观点明确并展开**：立场清晰（同意 Paul，提出 70% 表现 + 30% 努力的具体方案），并用"保护信心""教师职责""两类学生各得其所"支撑，展开达标。
> 2. **对讨论的贡献（高分关键）**：为 Paul 补充了新理由（防止挫败、发现潜能），但完全没有回应 Kelly——而 Kelly 的公平性质疑恰好直指 70/30 方案（答错的学生凭努力分仍能拿可观分数），这是本文上不了 4 分的最大原因。
> 3. **词数**：117 词（精确计数），达标。
> 4. **语言准确**：拼写 1 处（ethusiasm）、碎片句 1 处（Because 从句独立成句）、搭配与动词误用多处（admitting efforts、judge on、serve 70% grade、would better give、good at study）、可数名词裸用与不可数误用多处（satisfying grade、efforts），错误密度偏高，部分表达明显干扰理解，属积累性扣分。

### 第 2 句

## 结构逻辑评估

1. **论证链基本完整**：立场 → 理由一（保护信心）→ 理由二（教师职责）→ 方案（70/30）→ 效果（两类学生各得其所）。但第 4 句（发现潜能）到第 6 句（70/30 打分）之间有小跳跃：从"发现潜能"直接跳到"按比例打分"，中间缺"给努力定量打分正是发现潜能的方式"这一过渡。
2. **与 Kelly 零互动（最大短板）**：Kelly 的公平性质疑恰好击中 70/30 方案的要害——"答错的学生凭什么拿努力分"。作者本可用"努力分设 30% 上限、表现仍是主体"来回应，全文只字未提。只回应一位同学，按官方标准贡献度不足。
3. **70/30 方案的论证缺口**：未说明努力如何被客观衡量（谁来判定、标准是什么），这是 Kelly 潜在的追问点；也未解释为什么恰是 70/30 而非其他比例。
4. **扣题情况**：全程围绕 performance 与 effort 的权衡展开，立场明确，扣题良好。

> [!tip] 改进要点（按优先级）
> 1. **学会回应反方观点（最高优先级，直接决定能否上 4 分）**：Kelly 必须被回应。练习模板：先概括对方观点（Admittedly, Kelly has a point that...）→ 指出漏洞（However, ...）→ 一句因果论证支撑（because...）。
> 2. **消灭基础语言错误**：拼写（enthusiasm）、可数/不可数（effort 不可数、grade 需冠词）、碎片句（Because 从句不能独立成句）；建议建立个人易错清单，写完留 30 秒只查这三类。
> 3. **提升用词地道性**：serve 70% grade → give 70% of the grade；would better give → would do better to give；admit efforts → acknowledge efforts；good at study → good at studying。
> 4. **给方案补一句"如何操作"**：提出 70/30 这类具体方案时，追加一句说明努力如何衡量（如作业完成度、订正情况），论证更牢。
> 5. **把"同意"变成"贡献"**：同意 Paul 时必须追加新论据（本文已做到一半：防挫败论），再补一个 Paul 没说过的角度（如大学申请、职场评估同样看持续投入）。

# 范文

I agree with Paul that grades should reflect more than raw performance, and I'd even go further by proposing a concrete formula: 70% for results and 30% for effort. Admittedly, Kelly has a point that this could seem unfair to top performers. However, once the effort component is capped, results still decide the largest share of every grade, so strong students are never dragged down to the level of weaker ones. Capping effort also protects students' motivation: when a struggling student sees that steady work translates into visible progress, they are far less likely to give up. Real-world evaluation works the same way: universities and employers care about growth and persistence, not just final numbers. A student who improves from 40 to 60 points has not passed, but that progress deserves recognition. In short, measuring effort alongside results makes assessment both fairer and more encouraging.

**范文说明**：
1. 词数：145 词（精确计数）。
2. 对 Kelly 的回应方式：先用 Admittedly, Kelly has a point 概括其公平性担忧，再反驳——努力分设上限、表现仍决定成绩主体，优等生不会被拉低到差生水平。
3. 对 Paul 的补充论据：不复述其 character 论，改为两条新理由——努力分防止挫败、保护学习动机；大学与职场等真实评估同样看重成长与坚持。
4. 结构说明：立场句（同意 Paul + 具体 70/30 公式）→ 回应 Kelly → 两条支持 Paul 的新理由 → 40 分到 60 分的具体例子 → 收尾回扣。一个好段、顶格不分段，长短句交错，含一个缩写（I'd），符合半正式讨论帖语体。
5. 可学的好词好句：cap / be capped（设上限）；be dragged down to the level of（被拉低到……的水平）；translate into visible progress（转化为看得见的进步）；I'd even go further by proposing a concrete formula（再进一步提出具体方案）。
