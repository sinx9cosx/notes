## 一、冠词用法总览

冠词分三类：**不定冠词 a/an、定冠词 the、零冠词（不用冠词）**。核心原则：**可数名词单数通常不能“裸奔”**，前面要有冠词或其他限定词；复数/不可数名词泛指时常用零冠词，特指时用 the。

### 1. 不定冠词 a/an
用于**可数名词单数**，表示“一个/某一类”。

- **a + 辅音音素开头**：a book, a university, a European country  
- **an + 元音音素开头**：an apple, an hour, an honest man  
  > 看发音，不看字母：a university /juː/；an hour /aʊ/。

主要用法：
1. 第一次提到：I saw **a dog**. The dog was black.
2. 表示“一个”：She has **a car**.
3. 表示类别：**A dog** is a faithful animal.
4. 表示“每一”：twice **a week**
5. 某些固定搭配：**a lot of, a few, a little, have a cold, take a walk, in a hurry**

### 2. 定冠词 the
表示**特指、双方已知、独一无二**等。可用于可数单复数、不可数名词前。

主要用法：
1. 再次提到：I bought a book. **The book** is interesting.
2. 特指：**The man** over there is my teacher.
3. 独一无二：**the sun, the moon, the earth**
4. 最高级、序数词、only 等：**the best, the first, the only**
5. 江河湖海、山脉群岛、方位：**the Yangtze River, the Pacific, the Alps, the east**
6. 乐器：play **the piano**
7. 姓氏复数表一家：**the Smiths**
8. 形容词表一类人：**the rich, the poor**
9. 固定搭配：**in the morning/afternoon/evening, at the same time, on the other hand**

### 3. 零冠词
即不用冠词。常见于：
1. **复数名词泛指**：**Dogs** are loyal.
2. **不可数名词泛指**：**Water** is important.
3. 专有名词：**China, Tom, Beijing**
4. 季节、月份、星期：**spring, May, Monday**
5. 三餐、球类、棋类、学科、语言：**have breakfast, play football, study math, speak English**
6. by + 交通工具：**by bus, by bike**
7. 职位作补语：He was elected **president**.
8. 固定搭配：**go to school/hospital/church**（表目的）；**at home, in bed, on foot**

> 对比：**go to school** 去上学；**go to the school** 去学校这个地方。**in hospital** 住院；**in the hospital** 在医院里。

### 4. 冠词与单复数配合口诀
- **a/an + 可数单数**：a book
- **the + 可数单数/复数/不可数**：the book / the books / the water
- **零冠词 + 复数/不可数泛指**：books / water
- **可数单数一般不能单独出现**：不能说 I saw dog. 应说 I saw **a dog/the dog**.

### 5. 冠词位置
- all/both/half/double + **the** + 名词：all **the** students
- such/quite/what + **a/an** + adj. + 名词：such **a** good boy
- as/so/too/how + adj. + **a/an** + 名词：so good **a** boy

## 二、名词单复数用法

### 1. 先分可数/不可数
- **可数名词**：有单复数，可与 a/an 连用：a book → books
- **不可数名词**：一般无复数，不加 a/an，用量词：a piece of advice, two bottles of water

常见不可数：**advice, information, furniture, equipment, luggage, news, progress, homework, research, knowledge, bread, water, money**。  
> 不能说 an advice / advices，应说 **a piece of advice / some advice**。

### 2. 规则复数变化
1. 一般 +s：book→books
2. 以 s, x, ch, sh 结尾 +es：bus→buses, box→boxes, watch→watches
3. 辅音字母+y：变 y 为 i 加 es：city→cities；元音+y 直接加 s：boy→boys
4. 部分 f/fe → ves：leaf→leaves, knife→knives, wife→wives；但 roof→roofs, chief→chiefs
5. 部分 o 结尾 +es：potato→potatoes, tomato→tomatoes, hero→heroes；但 photo→photos, piano→pianos

### 3. 不规则复数
- man→men, woman→women, child→children
- foot→feet, tooth→teeth, mouse→mice, goose→geese
- ox→oxen
- 外来词：analysis→analyses, crisis→crises, phenomenon→phenomena, datum→data, medium→media

### 4. 单复同形
**sheep, deer, fish, means, series, species, aircraft**  
例：one sheep → two sheep；a series → two series。

### 5. 通常只用复数
**clothes, scissors, glasses, pants, trousers, goods, belongings, surroundings**  
> a pair of scissors / trousers / glasses

### 6. 形式是复数、意义常作单数
**news, maths, physics, politics, measles**  
例：The news **is** good. Maths **is** difficult.

### 7. 集合名词
- **people, police, cattle** 通常作复数：The police **are** coming.
- **family, team, class, group** 可单可复：作整体用单数；强调成员用复数。  
  My family **is** big. / My family **are** all here.

### 8. 复合名词复数
- 一般变主体：mother-in-law→mothers-in-law
- man/woman 修饰时前后都变：a man doctor→two **men doctors**
- 以 man/woman 结尾：policeman→policemen

### 9. 主谓一致提醒
- each/every/no + 单数名词 + 单数谓语
- **a number of + 复数** + 复数谓语；**the number of + 复数** + 单数谓语
- 分数/百分数 + of + 名词，谓语随名词：Two thirds of the water **is** clean. Two thirds of the students **are** here.
- there be 就近原则：There **is** a book and two pens.
