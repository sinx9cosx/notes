# question 2 
if you select a book for pleasure reading, would you prefer a work fiction or non-fiction book? why?

I prefer a fiction. As for me, reading fictions can be an interesting thing to do when I dive deeply into the story and follow the characters' action. It helps me to better understand the world because literature comes from the real life. Additionally, I  admire author's creativity and enjoy the wonderful world without pressure. So reading fictions is really a relaxing choice for me.
# question 3

I don't think that digital books would totally take palce of printed books. Firstly, printed books are need in school for education. Secondly, many readers still love printed books, turning pages makes people feel more real about books and imerged. So in conclusion, I think printed books won't disappear.
