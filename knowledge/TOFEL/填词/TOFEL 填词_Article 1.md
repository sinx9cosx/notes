# 题目

Many animals use specific behaviors to communicate with others in their group. For example, bees perform a "waggle dance" to **show** the **location** of food **sources** to their hive. Birds often **sing** complex **songs** to attract **mates** or defend their **territory**. Some animals, like dolphins, rely on **vocal** clicks and **whistles** to coordinate group **activities**. These behaviors are essential for survival, as they help animals find food, avoid predators, and reproduce successfully. Understanding these actions provides valuable insights into the social structures of different species.
