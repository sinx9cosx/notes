# 题目

You recently had a conversation with your friend Sarah, who is considering a new job opportunity in another city. Sarah is unsure about making the decision and asked for advice. In the past, you have discussed her career goals and personal priorities together, and now you want to provide thoughtful advice based on her current situation.  
  
Write an email to Sarah. In your email, do the following:  
- Explain your perspective on her situation.  
- Offer specific advice to help her make a decision.  
- Encourage her to weigh her options.

Dear Sarah, 
I hope you're doing well.Hearing that you're considering about a new job opportunity,I'm happy to tell you my advice.
I remember that you always hold a positive attitude toward career,this new job offers a just right road to achieve your goal.For your social life,it's no need to worry because you can communicate with friends online convieniently.Also we can go to see you in free time.

# 审题（问题）


虽然三项任务都有涉及，但完成得还不够充分，主要问题如下：

## （1）“Explain your perspective”部分较简略

你提到：

- you always hold a positive attitude toward career
- this new job offers a just right road to achieve your goal

这说明了你支持她换工作的原因，但对“她为什么犹豫”分析不够。题干强调她“is unsure”，所以如果能写出她面临的两难，会更贴合题目。

## （2）“Offer specific advice”不够具体

你目前的建议主要是：

- 接受机会
- 适应新环境
- 提升技能
- 认识新朋友

这些方向是对的，但还不够“specific”。更具体的建议应包括：

- 比较新旧工作的优缺点
- 考虑职业发展与个人生活是否平衡
- 想清楚自己最看重什么

## （3）“Encourage her to weigh her options”写得较弱

你写了：

- I hope you can consider it carefully as it is a big deal for you.
- No matter what your decision is, I'll always support you.

这是鼓励，但“weigh her options”更强调**比较不同选择**。目前缺少对“留在原地”和“去新城市”两个选项的明确权衡。


你可以补充类似下面的内容，使任务完成更充分：

- I think it is natural to feel unsure because this decision affects both your career and your personal life.
- You should compare the advantages of the new job with the comfort and stability of staying where you are now.
- Before deciding, make a list of the pros and cons, such as career growth, salary, lifestyle, and distance from friends and family.

# 范文

高分写作要做到三点：

明确表达观点
先说明你对她目前情况的看法，比如这是一个很好的机会，但也需要认真考虑，因为换城市不仅影响工作，也影响生活。

提供具体建议
结合她的职业目标和个人 priorities，建议她比较新工作和现在工作的优缺点，例如职业发展、薪水、生活成本、离家人朋友的距离，以及她是否喜欢新城市。

鼓励权衡选择
最后提醒她不要急着决定，可以列出利弊，想清楚什么对她最重要，再作出选择。语气要友好、支持性强，符合邮件风格。

To: Sarah
Subject: Advice on Your Job Opportunity Decision
Hi Sarah,
I hope you are doing well. I have been thinking about our conversation, and I believe this job opportunity could be a great step for you. Since you have always wanted career growth, this new position may help you reach your goals.
At the same time, moving to another city is a big decision. I think you should compare the benefits and challenges carefully. For example, consider the salary, work environment, cost of living, and how comfortable you feel about leaving your current life behind.
It may help to make a list of pros and cons. Take your time and choose what fits your goals and personal priorities best.
Thank you,
