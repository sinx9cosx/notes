# 无限深势阱中的粒子

1. Solve the Schrodinger Equation (SE) of particle in a well (PIW) using complex exponential function.
$$
\psi(x)=Ae^{ikx}+Be^{-ikx}
$$

2. Show that the complex conjugate of the solution you find in question 1 is also the solution of the SE for PIW.

3. Can you make a general conclusion, that if $\psi(x)$ is a solution of any SE, it complex conjugate is also the solution of that SE? What condition you need?

4. Show that if question3 is true, any linear combination of the solution of the SE $\psi(x)$ and $\psi*(x)$ is also the solution of the SE.

5. Prove that if $V(x)$ is an even function (that is $V(-x)=V(x)$) then $\psi(x)$ can always be taken to be either even or odd.
