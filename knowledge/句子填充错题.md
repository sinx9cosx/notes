A：Why did you move into a new dorm?  
B：The one I was living in had maintenance issues.
错误：I was living in the one had maintenance issues.
讲解：主干是 The one had maintenance issues；"(that) I was living in" 是修饰 the one 的定语从句（that 作 in 的宾语，可省略）。错误版把两个句子硬拼在一起，导致 had 缺少主语。


A：Why did you miss the team meeting yesterday?  
B：I was studying at the library for my exam.
错误：I was studying for my exam at the library.
讲解：多个状语并列时地点在前、目的在后，应为 studying at the library（地点）+ for my exam（目的）；反序易被误读为"考试在图书馆举行"。
