## 错题

The line at the registrar seemed to take forever.
A：That class meets every other day.
B：Were you able to get your other tasks completed?
C：There are a lot of lines to memorize for the play.
D：Does the bookstore close early today?

我的答案：C
正确答案：B
解析：原句说在注册处（registrar）排队（line）排了很久，B 顺着该语境问"那你其他任务还完成得了吗"，最贴合；C 是干扰项，借 line 的另一义"台词"（the play 的台词）误导，与原文无关。

When does enrollment for next semester open?
A：Spring registration was a mess last year.
B：Senior students get priority slots.
C：Probably listed on the registrar's page.
D：Tuition deposits are due in three weeks.
我的答案：D
正确答案：C
解析：问的是下学期注册何时开放，C 说"时间应该公布在注册处的页面上"，是唯一给出查询途径的回答；D 说的是学费押金截止日期，答非所问。

I didn't mean to bail on our study session last time.
A：Sure, anytime.
B：I'm sorry to hear that!
C：Next time would be great!
D：No worries.
我的答案：C
正确答案：D
解析：对方说"上次不是故意爽约的"（bail on = 退出/爽约），实质是在道歉，D "No worries（没事）" 是接受道歉的得体回应；C "下次就好了" 不合道歉语境。

It's really noisy in the lobby, isn't it?​
A：Yes, I turned mine down.​
B：I haven't been able to work with them yet.​
C：They don't know how to do it.​
D：It's the first day of the big medical conference.​
我的答案：A
正确答案：D
解析：对方说大厅很吵并寻求认同（isn't it），D 给出原因"今天是大型医学会议第一天"，自然合理；A 的 turned mine down（把"我的"调小声）看似相关，但 mine 无所指、与大厅噪音无关，是干扰项。
